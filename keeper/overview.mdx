The keeper is an off-chain Rust service that the protocol runs. It pushes a reference price for each market, manages the 26 bin books, settles committed orders, moves inventory, and posts per-market parameters such as fees and the γ index. Its source code and binary are not published; these pages describe how the protocol's keeper behaves and how it is configured.

Most loops have an environment switch, but not all. γ posting has no switch of its own and stops only if the whole book manager is turned off. Lending collateral, which happens only on mainnet, and the legacy Meteora layer, with its arbitrage loop, ship turned off. See [Keeper configuration](/keeper/running) for variables and defaults.

## What it runs

| Task | Cadence | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Price push** | 5 s in the deployed image (`UPDATE_INTERVAL_MS`, code default 15 s) | Fetches each market's reference price, checks it against Hyperliquid, and writes it with mint-redeem `update_risk_state_v2` to `Market.user_twap_price` (a spot price despite the name, 1e6, corporate-action adjusted) and `twap_updated_at`. If the source price is over 120 s old or the fetch fails, it withholds the push. 300 s after the last push, mint and redeem revert with `TwapNotAvailable` and the books switch to free float. |
| **Book manager** | 30 s | Re-centres each book on its inventory-shifted centre once it drifts past the reposition gate, and posts the bin shape with `set_pool_shape`. See [Book management](/keeper/book-management). |
| **Inventory** | With the book manager | Trims asks above 1.5× the bid target. Each side's bid cUSDC stays at the depth its next free-float book will post (\$500 today): the excess is swept to the CLP vault, and bids that sellers spent are refilled from it (`clp_fund_pool_usdc`). |
| **Parameter posting** | With the book manager | Posts the mint and redeem fee, `clamp(fee_mult × half-spread, 10, 100)` bps (`update_market`), the committed-order spread, `clamp(half-spread, 10, 500)` bps (`set_commit_spread`), each market's fixed overcollateralization target (`set_buffer_target`, only when it differs from the value on chain) and the γ index (`set_s_gamma_index`). Unposted γ is saved to R2 or `DATA_DIR` and survives a restart, and a market with no γ write for a day is posted once at least 1e-6 is pending. |
| **Commit settler** | 20 s idle, 5 s while orders are pending | Finds pending committed orders and settles up to 10 per tick, largest first, simulating each settle first. A refund or a fill the CLP vault covers is sent as it is. A fill short of cash borrows from the treasury (`allocate_to_market`) and mints or redeems pairs if needed, in the same transaction as the settle where it fits, so a fill that fails takes its funding with it. Orders held back by the collateral floor or the oracle checks wait for a later push. It refunds expired orders about 5 s after expiry (`cancel_swap`). `settle_swap` is permissionless; the keeper is the cranker that is always running. |
| **Liquidity reallocator** | 30 s | Keeps each market's CLP vault between its working need and twice it: what its bid refills can spend in one tick, \$1,250 per market today (2 × \$500 × 1.25). Tops up from the treasury below the need and returns everything above 1.5× once a vault passes twice the need (`allocate_to_market`, `return_to_global`). |
| **Treasury backstop** | With the reallocator | Tops up a market's collateral from the treasury (`backstop_market`): at once below 100% at the stored price, and after 5 minutes short of its posted target by more than one confidence band. Fills to the target, at most 130% by default, and never takes a fill back. See [Solvency → Treasury backstop](/concepts/solvency#treasury-backstop). |
| **Cross-market rebalance** | With the book manager | Moves collateral from markets above their target ratio to markets below it (`rebalance_collateral`). It plans at the stored price the program checks and leaves the source one confidence band of margin. A destination within one band of its target is left alone, and past it is filled to target plus the band. Markets below 100% are filled first, and each source's excess is split in proportion to need. The program clamps each move to what the source holds above its own target, so a plan made at an old price moves less than requested and never drains the source. |
| **Redemption settler** | 60 s idle, 10 s while requests are pending | When `REDEMPTION_SETTLE_ENABLED` is true (default off), pays queued `request_redemption` requests in parts: from the vault's cash, with a recall from lending venues in the same transaction, and with a treasury advance for the rest. It repays an advance from the market's next recall. See [Redeem](/flows/redeem#when-the-vault-cannot-pay-yet). |
| **Devnet treasury trim** | 5 min | Devnet only, and only when `DEVNET_TREASURY_TARGET_USD` is set: withdraws what the treasury policy's allowance allows, never past the target, and burns it. |
| **Health** | 300 s | Watches the SOL balances of both signers. Below a critical line it tops the wallet up (a devnet airdrop, a mainnet USDC-to-SOL swap of at most \$250, or a transfer from the other signer) and posts a Discord alert if the wallet is still below. |

The **yield allocator** runs every 300 s. It plans how much of each market's collateral to lend, and where, from live mainnet state of three USDC venues (Kamino, Save and Jupiter Lend Earn; MarginFi is disabled). It lends only on mainnet with `COLLATERAL_YIELD_ENABLED=true`, which is off by default, and elsewhere it only plans. On devnet, an emulator credits each market the interest that plan would earn. See [Keeper → Yield allocator](/concepts/keeper#yield-allocator-300s).

Every transaction that moves funds is first written to a journal in R2 or on a persistent disk. If a send ends with an unknown outcome, the keeper pauses those sends until it has reconciled the transaction and restarted. Price pushes skip the journal because repeating one is harmless.

## What it does not do

- **Set prices by hand.** Each half-spread is the largest of four terms: a realized q90 ratchet, a slow auto-floor based on 60-day volatility, oracle confidence plus a fixed cost, and a static per-market floor. No trained model, no discretionary input. See [the keeper concept page](/concepts/keeper).
- **Sit in the user's call path.** `mint_paired`, `redeem_paired`, `pool_swap`, `commit_swap` and `cancel_swap` need neither keeper key. Mint and redeem do need a price push from the last 300 s. If the keeper stops, its last price ages past 300 s, and outside `Normal` the books then trade at their last posted bins. Anyone, the owner included, can call `cancel_swap` for an unsettled committed order once its 180 s TTL has passed, and the escrow goes back to the owner.

## Signing roles

The keeper wallet is each market's `keeper_authority`. It pushes prices, posts buffer targets and the γ index, rebalances collateral, and can call the keeper-only single-leg instructions (`keeper_mint_single`, `keeper_redeem_single`, `keeper_burn_single`). It could also move collateral to a keeper-owned account for lending (`deploy_collateral_to_yield`) on a market with `yield_enabled` set. Every live market has it set as of 11 Oct 2026, but the keeper lends only on mainnet.

The CLP authority signs book shapes, sweeps, inventory moves and `set_commit_spread`. Fee posting goes through `update_market`, which requires the market's `authority`.
