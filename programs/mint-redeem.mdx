title: mint-redeem
description: The Market account, paired mint and redeem at NAV, the keeper's price and γ writes, and market administration, grouped by who can call each instruction.

The mint-redeem program holds one `Market` account per synthetic asset, mints and burns its L and S tokens, and keeps the market's collateral ledger. Users call `mint_paired` and `redeem_paired`. The keeper writes the price, the confidence band, γ and the buffer target. The market authority configures and maintains the market.

Devnet ID: 5MBjhNUUguLTPNR5WG6YBUUw7vUcxQ14ARw3NsS3rKu4

## Accounts

### Market account

```
seeds = [b"market", asset_symbol]   // raw symbol bytes, e.g. "QQQ"
```

The account is at most 899 bytes: an 8-byte discriminator plus `Market::INIT_SPACE`, which reserves room for a 10-byte symbol and five `collateral_vaults` entries. `collateral_vaults` is a variable-length `Vec` (empty on every live market), so the fields after it have no fixed byte offsets. Decode the account with the IDL rather than reading at offsets. Devnet markets are allocated 891 bytes (10 Oct 2026); the data fits because the list is empty.

Fields in IDL order. Prices and amounts are 1e6 fixed point (6 decimals).

| Field | Type | Meaning |

| `authority` | Pubkey | Market authority. Signs the admin instructions; set to the `initialize_market` signer. |
| `fee_recipient` | Pubkey | Owner of the cUSDC account that receives the dev tax (`devTokenAccount`). |
| `asset_symbol` | String | Ticker, at most 10 bytes. The PDA seed. |
| `oracle_address` | Pubkey | Account that mint and redeem require as `oracle`. On devnet it is the market's `OracleConfig` PDA. |
| `oracle_type` | OracleType | `Pyth`, `Switchboard` or `Continuum`. `Switchboard` is rejected; live markets use `Continuum`. |
| `long_mint` | Pubkey | L token mint. |

| `collateral_mint` | Pubkey | Collateral mint (cUSDC on devnet). |

### RedemptionRequest

```
seeds = [b"redemption", market, owner]          // 223 bytes
escrows = [b"redemption_escrow", request, mint]   // one token account per leg
```

One open request per owner and market. `request_redemption` creates it and moves the owner's L and S into two escrow token accounts owned by the request PDA. Fields: `owner`, `market`, `long_mint`, `short_mint`, `l_requested`, `s_requested`, `l_remaining`, `s_remaining`, the running totals `gross_paid`, `net_paid`, `fee_paid` and `dev_tax_paid`, `fills`, `created_at`, `last_fill_at` and the three bumps. The request closes, and its rent goes back to the owner, when it is paid in full or cancelled.

### MarketAdvance

```
seeds = [b"market_advance", market]               // 123 bytes
```

The ledger of the treasury's loan to one market, created by `configure_market_advance`. Fields: `market`, `lender` (the treasury vault, the only account an advance can come from), `max_advance_bps` (0 turns advances off), `outstanding`, `total_advanced`, `total_repaid`, `total_written_off`, `last_advance_at`, `last_repay_at` and `bump`. Advance cash sits in the market's collateral vault but is not counted in `total_collateral`, so the collateral ratio and the redeem haircut never see it.

### The `collateral_authority` slot

Every market keeps its collateral in its own vault, owned by its Market PDA, and the Market PDA signs every payout. The upgrade of 11 Oct 2026 removed the unified vault from the program, along with the `CollateralAuthority` account type.

Six instructions still have a `collateral_authority` account in its old position:

- `redeem_paired`
- `keeper_mint_single` and `keeper_redeem_single`
- `redeem_single_for_vault`
- `withdraw_excess_to_clp` and `deploy_collateral_to_yield`

It is now an ignored optional slot. The program never reads it and accepts any account. Pass mint-redeem's program ID there: in Anchor that is `collateralAuthority: null` in TypeScript or `collateral_authority: None` in a Rust CPI.

The four unified-vault instructions are gone: `init_collateral_authority`, `migrate_to_unified_vault`, `exit_unified_vault` and `sweep_unified_surplus`. Calling one now fails with Anchor error 101 (`InstructionFallbackNotFound`).

## Pricing and fees

### Price source

In `ProxyMode`, `Stress` and `Recovery`, mint and redeem price from `user_twap_price` and revert with `TwapNotAvailable` when it is 0 or `twap_updated_at` is more than 300 s old. The keeper doesn't push while the underlying market is closed, so mint and redeem revert until it reopens. In `Normal` the program reads the oracle account itself. A Pyth feed must then be at most 60 s old, with a confidence interval within 2% of the price; a `Continuum` `OracleConfig` must be unpaused, `Active` and fresh. No market runs in `Normal` today.

### NAV and worst-case quotes

With P the price above, L = P and S = K / P × γ, where K = `initial_l_price × initial_s_price` and γ = `s_gamma_index` / 1e9.

Mint and redeem quote each leg against the user. Let c be `oracle_confidence` times the state's confidence multiplier: 1.5× in `ProxyMode`, 3× in `Stress`, 1× in `Normal`, and in `Recovery` 3× falling to 1× over the hour after the market enters `Recovery` (`risk_state_updated_at`). A mint prices L at P + c and S at K / (P − c) × γ. A redeem prices L at P − c and S at K / (P + c) × γ. Worst-case quoting is on by default, and `configure_worst_case_quoting` can switch it off per market; as of 10 Oct 2026 it is off on QQQ.

### Fees

The charged fee is `mint_fee_bps` or `redeem_fee_bps` times the state's fee multiplier: 1× in `Normal`, 1.5× in `ProxyMode`, 3× in `Stress`, and in `Recovery` 3× falling to 1× over the hour after the market enters `Recovery` (`risk_state_updated_at`). No waiver or discount applies, and donating earns none. The keeper posts both fees through `update_market` as `clamp(fee_mult × half-spread, 10, 100)` bps, using its own half-spread estimate for the market (`fee_mult` defaults to 1.0). As of 10 Oct 2026 the posted fees are QQQ 18, SPY 16, NVDA 31 and EUR, GBP and JPY 10 bps, so QQQ is charged 27 bps in `ProxyMode`.

The mint fee comes out of the deposit; the redeem fee comes out of the gross payout after any haircut. `dev_tax_pct` (10%) of each fee goes to the fee recipient's cUSDC account, the treasury on every live market since 12 Oct 2026. The rest stays in the vault and counts in `total_collateral`.

### Limits

Mint and redeem each need at least 10 cUSDC of value (`BelowMinimum`). `Stress` blocks both by default, and every live market keeps that default. `stress_mint_cap` applies only in `Stress` and is 0 (no cap).

## Instructions

Grouped by the signer the program checks. Anchor TS account names are camelCase versions of the IDL names (`user_long_account` becomes `userLongAccount`).

### Anyone

#### `mint_paired(collateral_amount: u64)`

Deposits `collateral_amount` cUSDC (at least 10 cUSDC) and mints L and S to the user, half of the net value to each leg at the mint NAVs. Accounts: `market`, `user`, `userCollateral`, `userLongAccount`, `userShortAccount`, `longMint`, `shortMint`, `collateralVault`, `devTokenAccount`, `oracle`, `tokenProgram`. The three user token accounts must already exist. See the [mint flow](/flows/mint).

#### `redeem_paired(l_amount: u64, s_amount: u64)`

Burns the largest value-balanced pair inside `l_amount` and `s_amount` and pays `l × L + s × S` for the pair it burns, at the redeem NAVs, less the fee. Both amounts must be above 0, and they are maxima. The balanced pair is all of the leg worth less at the worst-case redeem NAVs, and the same value of the other leg, rounded down. The rest of the larger leg stays in the caller's wallet. The accounts are those of `mint_paired` plus the ignored `collateralAuthority` slot; pass `null`. If the market's collateral ratio at those NAVs is under 100%, every redeemer is paid `nominal × collateral ratio`, so early redeemers can't drain the vault ahead of others. Above 100% the haircut does nothing. If the payout is larger than the market's liquid collateral (`total_collateral` minus `deployed_to_yield`) or the vault's balance, it fails with `InsufficientLiquidCollateral` (6022). Queue the redemption with `request_redemption` instead. See the [redeem flow](/flows/redeem).

The balanced burn closes audit finding MR-2. A lopsided paired redeem, for example one atom of L plus all S right after a mint, used to be an instant one-leg sale at the stored price, so a trader who saw the next price push coming could sell the falling leg before it landed. Details:

- An offer already balanced to within one base unit of the dearer leg's NAV is burned exactly as sent, so the rule is idempotent.
- If the balanced pair has a zero leg, it fails with `InvalidAmount`. One atom of L worth less than one base unit of S is an example.
- The pair it burns must be worth at least 10 cUSDC, or it fails with `BelowMinimum`.
- When it trims, it logs `Balanced pair: burning X L + Y S of the A L + B S offered; the rest stays with the redeemer`. The `PairedActionExecuted` event and the `Redeemed paired tokens` log report the amounts actually burned.
- The CLP redeeming its own inventory through `vault_redeem_pairs` (the CLP PDA signs) is exempt. It burns exactly what it asks.
- A balanced burn moves traders' net position by less than \$1, so redemptions take no exposure remaining accounts and can no longer fail with `ExposureCapExceeded` (6060) or `ExposureAccountsMissing` (6062). These codes are raised only by the redemption check, so they can no longer occur in practice. They stay in the enum and the check stays in the code as a guard. The cap errors on the swap paths (`pool_swap`, `settle_swap`) belong to the CLP program, as 6045 and 6046. See the [exposure cap](/concepts/solvency#6-exposure-cap).

The instruction's arguments and accounts did not change. Only the doc comments in the IDL did. The change shipped on devnet on 14 Oct 2026 (slot 511012606).

#### `donate_to_vault(amount: u64)`

Adds cUSDC to a market's collateral without minting anything. The market must be active and `amount` above 0. The amount goes into the collateral vault and `total_collateral`, and the donor gets nothing back: no tokens, no fee waiver and no other reward. It reads no price, so it has no staleness check. Accounts: `market`, `collateralVault`, `donorCollateral`, `donor` (signer), `tokenProgram`. The app stopped offering donations on 9 Oct 2026.

#### `request_redemption(params: RedemptionAmounts)`

Queues a paired redemption the vault cannot pay now. `RedemptionAmounts` holds `l_amount` and `s_amount`, both above 0. It runs the same checks as `redeem_paired` (market active, risk state, price freshness, the 10 cUSDC minimum) and applies the same balanced-pair rule, then moves only the balanced pair into the request's escrow token accounts and creates the [`RedemptionRequest`](#redemptionrequest). Nothing is burned or paid yet. Signer: the owner. Accounts: `market`, `owner`, `request`, `escrowL`, `escrowS`, `ownerLongAccount`, `ownerShortAccount`, `longMint`, `shortMint`, `oracle`, `tokenProgram`, `systemProgram`. Emits `RedemptionRequested`. The rest of the larger leg stays with the owner. `l_requested`, `s_requested` and the event's amounts are the escrowed, balanced amounts, and `settle_redemption` burns the escrow pro rata, so a request stays balanced. A request takes no exposure remaining accounts, and `settle_redemption` does not check the cap. See the [redeem flow](/flows/redeem#when-the-vault-cannot-pay-yet).

#### `cancel_redemption()`

Returns the unpaid part of a request, with all rent, and closes it. Signer: the owner. It reads no `Market`, so it works while the market is paused, the price is stale or the keeper is down. It returns each escrow's actual balance, so atoms sent to an escrow by someone else can't block the close. Emits `RedemptionCancelled`.

#### `permissionless_burn(is_long: bool, amount: u64)`

Burns L or S from the signer's own token account with no payout, which lowers the supply ledger. A delegate can't call it on someone else's account. The CLP's `clp_burn_excess` uses it to retire inventory.

### Setup

#### `initialize_market(params: InitMarketParams)`

`InitMarketParams { asset_symbol, oracle_type, initial_l_price, initial_s_price, mint_fee_bps, redeem_fee_bps, keeper_authority }`. Only the mint-redeem program's upgrade authority can call it: the instruction takes the program and its `program_data` as its last two accounts and fails with `UnauthorizedInitializer` (6046) for any other signer. The signer becomes `market.authority` and `fee_recipient`. The symbol must be at most 10 bytes, both fees at most 1,000 bps, and both prices above 0. `oracle_type` must be `Pyth` or `Continuum`, and for `Continuum` the `oracle` account must be the market's `OracleConfig` PDA. The instruction doesn't create the L and S mints or the vault: they must already exist, with the Market PDA as mint authority of both mints. A new market starts in `Normal` with `user_twap_price = initial_l_price`, `dev_tax_pct` 10, the default risk-fee and worst-case settings, and a 1/1 price adjustment. It also sets `yield_enabled = true` and leaves `yield_ceiling_bps` at 0, so harvests can run at once but nothing can be deployed to a lending venue until `configure_yield_ceiling` raises the ceiling. The full sequence is in [listing a market](/programs/overview#listing-a-market-operator-process).

### Keeper (signer = `market.keeper_authority`)

#### `update_risk_state_v2(new_risk_state: RiskState, user_twap_price: u64, oracle_confidence: u64, source_publish_time: i64)`

The keeper's price write. It maps the price and the confidence through the corporate-action ratio, then stores them with the risk state and sets `twap_updated_at` to `source_publish_time`. It rejects a source time in the future, more than 300 s old or before the latest corporate-action boundary. It also rejects a price of 0, a move of more than 25% from the stored price, and any move out of `Stress` except to `Recovery`. `update_risk_state(new_risk_state, user_twap_price, oracle_confidence)` does the same without a source time and stamps `twap_updated_at` with the chain time.

#### `set_s_gamma_index(params: SetGammaParams)`

`SetGammaParams { s_gamma_index: u64, expected_epoch: u32 }` posts γ at 1e9 scale. Bad writes are rejected, never clamped: a value outside 0.5 to 1.0 (`GammaOutOfRange`), a value above the current γ (`GammaMustRatchetDown`), or an `expected_epoch` other than `gamma_epoch` (`GammaEpochMismatch`). There is no per-write or per-day cap. γ is always on; the keeper refuses to boot if `CLMM_GAMMA_DECAY_ENABLED` is set.

#### `set_buffer_target(buffer_target_bps: u16)`

Posts the market's buffer over parity, clamped to 100 to 5,000 bps (a target ratio of 101% to 150%). The keeper posts each market's daily Monte Carlo target, and only when it differs from the stored one; see [Solvency](/concepts/solvency#3-posted-overcollateralization-buffer). The target gates how much excess a market can shed (`withdraw_excess_to_clp`, `rebalance_collateral`). Mints and redeems don't read it.

#### `settle_redemption(params: SettleRedemptionParams)`

Pays the largest part of a request that the vault's cash covers now, up to `max_l_amount` of L with the matching share of S. Prices, haircut, fee and dev tax work exactly as in `redeem_paired`. It burns that part from escrow, pays the owner and updates the request. Unlike `redeem_paired`, it may spend the cash of a treasury advance, but never more than the vault holds. It fails with `NothingToSettle` (6053) when the request is empty or no part of it fits. When the request is paid in full and the owner is passed, the escrows and the request close and the rent goes back to the owner. Emits `RedemptionSettled` and `PairedActionExecuted`.

#### `keeper_mint_single`, `keeper_redeem_single`, `keeper_burn_single`

`(is_long: bool, collateral_amount: u64)` for the mint and `(is_long: bool, token_amount: u64)` for the other two. They are a fee-free single-leg mint and redeem at the base NAV, with no worst-case band and no minimum, and a burn with no payout. The mint must leave the collateral ratio at or above the lower of its pre-trade value and 100% (`SingleSideMintStressFloor`). The redeem must leave it at or above 100%. Users have no single-leg redeem.

#### `rebalance_collateral(amount: u64)`

Moves up to `amount` of collateral from one market's vault to another's. The signer must be the keeper of both markets, and the two markets must differ (`InvalidAmount`). The move is capped at the smallest of the request, the source's collateral above its own target ratio at the stored price, and its liquid collateral (`total_collateral − deployed_to_yield`). A request above the cap moves the capped amount, and the log line shows both figures. It fails only when the source has nothing above target (`InsufficientCollateral`) or no liquid collateral (`InsufficientLiquidCollateral`). The source's price must be at most 300 s old.

The move is a plain token transfer from the source market's vault to the other market's vault, signed by the source Market PDA. If the two markets share a vault, the transfer would move nothing while both ledgers changed, so the program refuses it (`InvalidAmount`).

#### `deploy_collateral_to_yield`, `recall_collateral_from_yield`, `harvest_collateral_yield`

`(amount: u64, _protocol: u8)` for deploy and recall, `(yield_amount: u64)` for harvest. Deploy and recall move collateral to and from a lending venue through the keeper's token account. A deployment must stay within `yield_ceiling_bps` of `total_collateral`.

Harvest moves `yield_amount` from the keeper's token account into `market.collateral_vault` and adds it to `total_collateral` and `total_yield_harvested`, so harvested yield raises the market's collateral ratio. Collateral above the target can still reach the CLP through `withdraw_excess_to_clp`. Harvest's fourth account was named `clp_vault` and received the yield until 11 Oct 2026. It is now `collateral_vault`, in the same position, and must be the market's own vault.

Every live market has `yield_enabled = true`. Nothing is deployed on devnet: the keeper's emulator calls harvest every hour with cUSDC minted at the rate its mainnet lending plan would earn.

### CLP PDA (CPI only)

#### `redeem_single_for_vault(is_long: bool, token_amount: u64)`

Like `keeper_redeem_single`, but it burns from CLP-owned token accounts and pays the market's CLP vault. The signer must be the CLP PDA `[b"clp", market]`, so it runs only through the CLP program's `vault_redeem_single`.

### Market authority (signer = `market.authority`)

#### `update_market(mint_fee_bps?, redeem_fee_bps?, is_active?, dev_tax_pct?)`

Fees at most 1,000 bps each; `dev_tax_pct` at most 25. The keeper posts fees through this instruction.

#### `update_keeper_authority(new_keeper_authority)`, `update_market_oracle(oracle_type)`, `update_fee_recipient(new_recipient)`

Rotate the keeper signer, repoint the oracle account (validated like `initialize_market`), or change the fee recipient.

#### `configure_risk_fees(params: ConfigureRiskFeesParams)`

`ConfigureRiskFeesParams { proxy_fee_multiplier, stress_fee_multiplier, stress_mint_cap, allow_mint_in_stress, allow_redeem_in_stress }`, every field optional. Sets the fields of `risk_fee_config`; a field left `None` is unchanged. Multipliers use 100 = 1×. The struct holds the separate arguments the instruction took until 11 Oct 2026, with the same fields in the same order, so the bytes on the wire are unchanged.

#### `configure_worst_case_quoting(enabled?, normal_multiplier?, proxy_multiplier?, stress_multiplier?)`

Sets `worst_case_config`. Multipliers use 100 = 1×.

#### `configure_yield_ceiling(ceiling_bps?, enabled?)`

Sets `yield_ceiling_bps` (at most 10,000) and `yield_enabled`.

#### `reanchor_gamma()`

Folds γ into the short anchor: `initial_s_price` becomes `floor(initial_s_price × γ)`, γ resets to 1.0 and `gamma_epoch` goes up by one. Every holder's claim stays the same at every price; the fold only resets how far γ can still fall. It does not reset the anchor to the spot price. Calls must be at least 14 days apart. Emits `GammaReanchored`. As of 10 Oct 2026 no market has been re-anchored.

#### `apply_corporate_action(params: CorporateActionParams)`

`CorporateActionParams { ratio_num, ratio_den, effective_ts }`, as new shares to old shares (a 2-for-1 split is 2:1). It folds the ratio into `price_adjust_num` and `price_adjust_den` and touches no price, supply or collateral. From then on each incoming keeper price is multiplied by the ratio before it is stored, which keeps P continuous across the split. An `effective_ts` at or before now applies immediately. A later time, up to 400 days ahead, arms the ratio for the ex-date; only one action can be armed at a time. Ratios beyond 1,000:1 are rejected. Emits `CorporateActionApplied`. As of 10 Oct 2026 no action is armed.

#### `withdraw_excess_to_clp(amount: u64)`

Moves collateral above the market's target ratio into the market's CLP vault. It needs a price at most 300 s old and moves only liquid collateral.

#### `mark_yield_impairment(amount: u64)`

Writes down a lost yield deployment by reducing both `deployed_to_yield` and `total_collateral`, so the redeem haircut sees the loss.

#### `configure_market_advance(params: ConfigureAdvanceParams)`

Sets the market's advance cap, `max_advance_bps` of `total_collateral` (at most 10,000; 0 turns advances off), and its lender, creating the [`MarketAdvance`](#marketadvance) on first use. The lender must be the CLP treasury vault, the cUSDC account of the `GlobalClp` PDA (`InvalidAdvanceLender`). It can change only while nothing is outstanding (`AdvanceLenderLocked`). Emits `MarketAdvanceConfigured`.

#### `receive_advance(amount: u64)`

Takes a treasury advance into the collateral vault. Both `market.authority` and the `funder` who owns the lender account sign; the CLP's [`advance_to_market`](/programs/clp#treasury) calls it with the `GlobalClp` PDA as funder. The cash is not added to `total_collateral`. The total outstanding can't pass the cap (`AdvanceCapExceeded`). Emits `AdvanceReceived`.

#### `repay_advance(amount: u64)`

Pays advance cash back from the vault to the lender. Signer: `market.authority` or the keeper. It can't repay more than is outstanding (`AdvanceRepayExceedsOutstanding`), and the vault must keep the market's own liquid collateral (`AdvanceWouldTakeOwnLiquidity`). Emits `AdvanceRepaid`.

#### `write_off_advance(amount: u64)`

Charges a venue loss to the outstanding advance first, because the treasury is first-loss capital. It lowers `deployed_to_yield` and `outstanding` by `amount` and moves no cash. A loss larger than the advance goes through `mark_yield_impairment`, which refuses while any advance is outstanding (`AdvanceOutstanding`). Emits `AdvanceWrittenOff`.

#### `create_token_metadata(name, symbol, uri)`

Creates Metaplex metadata for the market's L or S mint. The Market PDA signs as mint authority; the signer pays and becomes update authority. Limits: name 32 bytes, symbol 10, URI 200.

#### `realloc_market()`

Grows a `Market` account to 899 bytes after an upgrade that adds fields, with the signer paying the extra rent. It checks the owner, the discriminator and the `authority` stored at bytes 8 to 40. An account that is already large enough is left alone.

#### `close_market_admin()`

Zero-fills a market account and returns its lamports to the signer. For an account in the current `Market` layout it fails with `MarketNotEmpty` unless L supply, S supply, `total_collateral` and `deployed_to_yield` are all 0.

#### `fund_collateral(amount: u64)`

Tops up a market's collateral from the authority's own cUSDC account. It transfers `amount` into `market.collateral_vault` and adds it to `total_collateral`. Like `donate_to_vault`, it grants no fee waiver and has no price check. Emits `CollateralFunded`.

#### `fund_collateral_from(amount: u64)`

`fund_collateral` with a separate funder, so a program PDA such as the CLP treasury can fund a market through CPI. Added on 11 Oct 2026. It moves `amount` cUSDC from the funder's account into the market's vault, adds it to `total_collateral` and emits `CollateralFunded`. Accounts, in order:

| # | Account | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `market` (mut) | The Market PDA |
| 1 | `authority` (signer) | Must be `market.authority` (`Unauthorized`) |
| 2 | `funder` (signer) | Owns `funder_collateral`; can be a PDA signing through CPI |
| 3 | `collateral_vault` (mut) | Must be `market.collateral_vault` (`InvalidAmount`) |
| 4 | `funder_collateral` (mut) | Mint must be the collateral mint (`InvalidAmount`) and owner must be `funder` (`Unauthorized`) |
| 5 | `token_program` | SPL Token |

The CLP's [`backstop_market`](/programs/clp#treasury) calls it with the `GlobalClp` PDA as funder. The keeper sends that backstop when a market falls below 100% or stays short of its target.

#### `enable_multi_collateral()`, `add_collateral_vault(collateral_type, weight_bps)`, `update_collateral_vault(collateral_mint, is_active?, weight_bps?)`

Manage the `collateral_vaults` list. Mint and redeem don't read it, and it is empty on every market.

## Errors

mint-redeem errors run from 6000 to 6062. Codes 6029 and 6030 are reserved placeholders that are never raised. Codes 6048 to 6052 belonged to the unified vault and are no longer returned; they stay in the enum so later codes don't shift. Codes 6053 to 6059 come from the redemption requests and the treasury advance, and 6060 to 6062 from the exposure cap. Every code, with its message and cause, is in [Errors](/reference/errors#mint-redeem).

## TypeScript

Read a market's price, NAV and freshness. The IDL and its generated type come from [IDL files](/build/idls-and-pdas#idl-files).

```typescript
import { Program } from "@coral-xyz/anchor";
import { Connection, PublicKey } from "@solana/web3.js";
import idl from "./idl/mint_redeem.json" with { type: "json" };
import type { MintRedeem } from "./types/mint_redeem";

const connection = new Connection("https://api.devnet.solana.com", "confirmed");
const program = new Program<MintRedeem>(idl as MintRedeem, { connection });

const [marketPda] = PublicKey.findProgramAddressSync(
  [Buffer.from("market"), Buffer.from("QQQ")],
  program.programId,
);
const market = await program.account.market.fetch(marketPda);

const p = market.userTwapPrice.toNumber() / 1e6; // L NAV in cUSDC
const k = (market.initialLPrice.toNumber() / 1e6) * (market.initialSPrice.toNumber() / 1e6);
const gamma = market.sGammaIndex.isZero() ? 1 : market.sGammaIndex.toNumber() / 1e9;
const age = Math.floor(Date.now() / 1000) - market.twapUpdatedAt.toNumber();

console.log("risk state:", Object.keys(market.riskState)[0]);
console.log("L NAV:", p, "S NAV:", (k / p) * gamma);
console.log("price age:", age, "s", age > 300 ? "(mint and redeem revert)" : "");
```

These are base NAVs. Mint and redeem quote each leg against the user by the confidence band described in [pricing and fees](#pricing-and-fees). For a full mint and redeem script, see the [example](/build/example-mint-redeem).
