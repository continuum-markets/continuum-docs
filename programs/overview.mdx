Continuum runs on six programs built with Anchor 0.32.1. They are deployed on **devnet**; mainnet IDs will be added when mainnet launches.

| Program | Devnet ID | Role |
|---|---|---|
| [`mint-redeem`](/programs/mint-redeem) | `5MBjhNUUguLTPNR5WG6YBUUw7vUcxQ14ARw3NsS3rKu4` | One `Market` account per asset. Paired mint and redeem at NAV, the keeper's price, confidence and γ writes, and market administration. |
| [`clp`](/programs/clp) | `8xauDRjw9XRyk4FE3hW1JKjD8nC87gfr59Xig1dJqLES` | Protocol liquidity: the global vault (the protocol treasury) and the per-market vaults, committed orders (`commit_swap`, `settle_swap`, enabled on every live market), two bin books per market (`pool_swap`, which fills only while the market's price is stale) and the keeper's inventory instructions. |
| [`oracle`](/programs/oracle) | `5vxiCrDpFnQ2W5QtgZBC66K2XTC19bjVBjinGYYBsadC` | A per-market `OracleConfig` and Pyth feed registry. It is not updated on devnet; markets are priced from `Market.user_twap_price`. |
| [`registry`](/programs/registry) | `Hss7QZ46RcrV4ta63q2EM2Wf1Pzbjnd5FNjaTTDZHnau` | Symbol entries that point at mints and venues. Deployed and initialized, but it holds no markets and nothing reads it yet. |
| [`faucet`](/programs/faucet) (devnet only) | `9tUeQAPEtVSB68NSfvFAqfwaB74GuVxm6Zbp1hrMiNKY` | Drips 1,000 cUSDC per wallet every 24 h. |
| [`trading`](/flows/order-book) | `Anyy78cCJqapwvvFsignmQ45an5PCg9HfeDhDtAPNfmA` | Trading accounts over the Manifest order books. The owner's wallet funds and withdraws; a trading key in the browser places and cancels orders within a price band and a per-order limit, and can never withdraw. It reads `Market` for the band's centre and holds nothing but the traders' own book funds. |

A seventh program, governance (`6es5KcjMWKGhVWrttUytYiZ3YELXe4HQyrmsMVdbVawT`), is deployed on devnet but inactive. None of the six programs, the keeper or the app calls it, and it has no published IDL.

## How they relate

```
keeper ── update_risk_state_v2 ──► mint-redeem   Market.user_twap_price, oracle_confidence, risk_state
keeper ── set_pool_shape ────────► clp           reprices the two bin books of each market
clp ───── CPI ───────────────────► mint-redeem   mint_paired, redeem_paired,
                                                 redeem_single_for_vault, permissionless_burn,
                                                 fund_collateral_from (backstop_market),
                                                 receive_advance (advance_to_market)
clp ───── reads Market ──────────► mint-redeem   market state and NAV for commit_swap, settle_swap;
                                                 risk state and price age for pool_swap
oracle                                           one OracleConfig per market, not updated on devnet
registry                                         standalone and empty
faucet ── mints ─────────────────► cUSDC         devnet only
```

The keeper is the protocol's off-chain operator service; its source isn't published. mint-redeem never invokes the CLP program (`withdraw_excess_to_clp` only moves cUSDC into a CLP-owned token account). mint-redeem prices from the oracle account only in the `Normal` risk state, and every devnet market runs in `ProxyMode`, so devnet mint and redeem price from the keeper's write to `Market.user_twap_price`.

## IDLs

An IDL is the JSON description of a program's instructions, accounts, types, events and errors. The program source is private, so the IDLs are published in the docs repository instead, generated from the source of the deployed programs ([browse](https://github.com/continuum-markets/continuum-docs/tree/main/idl)). Each one is at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/continuum-markets/continuum-docs/main/idl/<program>.json`, where `<program>` is `mint_redeem`, `clp`, `oracle`, `registry`, `faucet` or `trading`. Anchor 0.32 reads the program ID from each IDL's `address` field.

[IDLs and PDAs](/build/idls-and-pdas#idl-files) has a download script and the TypeScript setup. For Rust CPI there is no published crate: load the same IDL with Anchor's `declare_program!` (see [CPI](/build/cpi)).

## Authority model

Each instruction that changes state checks its signer. The table follows the `has_one`, `address` and `require!` checks in the program source. `cancel_treasury_limits` and `freeze_treasury` accept either of two signers, so they appear in two rows.

| Signer | Instructions |
|---|---|
| Anyone | mint-redeem: `mint_paired`, `redeem_paired`, `mint_paired_with_waiver` and `redeem_paired_with_waiver` (with the signer's own waiver), `donate_to_vault`, `permissionless_burn` (owner of the burned account), `request_redemption` and `cancel_redemption` (the owner of the request). clp: `pool_swap`, `commit_swap`, `settle_swap` (any cranker), `cancel_swap` (anyone, after the order expires; the refund goes to the owner), `clp_swap` (retired; always fails), `deposit_profit` (the signer's own funds, paid into the treasury vault, which must be owned by the GlobalClp), `apply_treasury_limits` (once the pending change's delay has passed). faucet: `drip`. |
| `market.keeper_authority` | mint-redeem: `update_risk_state`, `update_risk_state_v2`, `set_s_gamma_index`, `set_buffer_target`, `keeper_mint_single`, `keeper_redeem_single`, `keeper_burn_single`, `rebalance_collateral` (keeper of both markets), `deploy_collateral_to_yield`, `recall_collateral_from_yield`, `harvest_collateral_yield`, `settle_redemption`, and `repay_advance` (the market authority may also call it). |
| `market.authority` | mint-redeem: `update_market`, `update_keeper_authority`, `update_market_oracle`, `update_fee_recipient`, `configure_risk_fees`, `configure_worst_case_quoting`, `configure_yield_ceiling`, `enable_multi_collateral`, `add_collateral_vault`, `update_collateral_vault`, `withdraw_excess_to_clp`, `mark_yield_impairment`, `reanchor_gamma`, `apply_corporate_action`, `create_token_metadata`, `realloc_market`, `close_market_admin`, `fund_collateral`, `fund_collateral_from` and `receive_advance` (each with a second signer, the `funder`, who owns the cUSDC), `configure_market_advance`, `write_off_advance`, `repay_advance` (the keeper may also call it). oracle: `initialize_oracle_config`. clp: `initialize_clp`. |
| mint-redeem program upgrade authority | mint-redeem: `initialize_market`. |
| CLP PDA `[b"clp", market]` | mint-redeem: `redeem_single_for_vault`, reachable only through the CLP's `vault_redeem_single`. |
| `clp.authority` (per market) | clp: `configure_swap`, `set_commit_spread`, `configure_inventory_budget`, `configure_yield_strategy`, `update_inventory`, `update_oi`, `update_oi_cap`, `collect_fees`, `record_deployment`, `record_withdrawal`, `vault_mint_pairs`, `vault_redeem_pairs`, `vault_redeem_single`, `clp_burn_excess`, `pool_sweep_usdc`, `pool_sweep_synth`, `clp_fund_pool_synth`, `clp_fund_pool_usdc`, and the legacy Meteora instructions (`configure_meteora_pools`, `clear_meteora_positions`, `clp_set_meteora_position`, `clp_init_bin_array`, `clp_open_meteora_position`, `clp_topup_meteora_position`, `clp_remove_meteora_liquidity`, `clp_claim_lp_fees`, `clp_close_meteora_position`). |
| `pool.authority` (per bin book) | clp: `set_pool_shape`, `configure_pool_geom`, `realloc_pool`. |
| `GlobalClp.authority` | clp: `initialize_global_clp`, `init_pool`, `admin_fund`, `admin_withdraw`, `allocate_to_market`, `return_to_global`, `add_yield_to_buffer`, `absorb_loss`, `configure_liquidity_seeder`, `init_revenue_attribution`, `configure_revenue_attribution`, `realloc_clp`, `record_harvest`, `vault_record_liquidity_recall`, `init_treasury_policy`, `set_treasury_limits`, `cancel_treasury_limits`, `freeze_treasury`, `init_treasury_yield`, `treasury_lend`, `treasury_recall`, `backstop_market` and `advance_to_market` (the last two need the signer to also be the market's `market.authority`). |
| `TreasuryPolicy.guardian` (freeze-only) | clp: `freeze_treasury`, `unfreeze_treasury` (guardian only), `cancel_treasury_limits`, `set_treasury_guardian` (guardian only). The guardian can't move funds. |
| `OracleConfig.admin_authority` | oracle: `update_oracle_config`, `configure_twap_windows`, `configure_health_thresholds`, `emergency_pause`, `upsert_oracle_feed`, `realloc_oracle_config`. |
| `OracleConfig.keeper_authority` | oracle: `update_price_observation`, `reset_volatility_counters`. |
| Registry authority | registry: `register_market`, `update_market`, `update_positions`, `set_active`, `transfer_authority`. |
| Faucet authority | faucet: `update_params`, `transfer_authority`. |
| Trading account owner | trading: `open_account`, `set_session`, `revoke_session`, `deposit`, `withdraw_sol`, `close_account`. |
| Owner or the account's active session key | trading: `withdraw` (pays only the owner's own token account), `claim_seat`, `deposit_to_market`, `withdraw_from_market`, `place_orders`, `swap_pool`, `commit_order`. A session key is bound by a price band and a per-order limit. |

The setup instructions (`initialize_*`, `init_*`) create program and market accounts. The Continuum team runs them when it lists a market; integrators never need them. The oracle's `get_*` instructions and the CLP's `check_inventory_restrictions` and `get_yield_status` are read-only views with no signer.

On devnet (10 Oct 2026) the Continuum team holds every admin role. `8xhPK1rGK4neoKo4fT8FxHQDZRiCnSYW9hvEdaVTxCni` is `market.authority` on all 14 markets (13 are live), and `9WXGdH5tPCprnATKxnScKt86TUZFYxqeMdejWRrp2RN4` is `market.keeper_authority` on every market. Both are stored on each `Market` account, so you can check them on chain.

## Choosing what to call

| Goal | Program | Instruction or account |
|---|---|---|
| Mint L and S | `mint-redeem` | `mint_paired` |
| Redeem L and S at NAV | `mint-redeem` | `redeem_paired`, with both amounts above 0 |
| Redeem more than the vault's cash can pay now | `mint-redeem` | `request_redemption`; the keeper pays it with `settle_redemption`, and `cancel_redemption` takes back the unpaid part |
| Read the price, NAV inputs and risk state | `mint-redeem` | fetch `Market` (`user_twap_price`, `twap_updated_at`, `oracle_confidence`, `s_gamma_index`) |
| Trade while the market is open, at NAV at a later keeper price push | `clp` | `commit_swap`, then `settle_swap`; `cancel_swap` refunds after the 180 s TTL |
| Trade instantly against a bin book while the market's price is stale | `clp` | `pool_swap` |
| Read book depth | `clp` | fetch the market's two `Pool` accounts |
| Get devnet cUSDC | `faucet` | `drip` |

There is no single-leg redeem: a lone L or S position exits through a committed order while the market is open, or through a bin book while its price is stale (see [Trade](/flows/trade)). `pool_swap` fails with `PoolClosedWhileLive` while the price is live. `clp_swap`, retired on 11 Oct 2026, reverts with `SwapDisabled` everywhere. Don't read `OracleConfig` for live prices, because the oracle program isn't updated on devnet.

## Listing a market (operator process)

Listing is an operator process; integrators get market addresses from [Addresses](/reference/addresses). The program calls run in this order, because each step needs accounts the previous one created.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the mints and the vault">
    Create the L and S mints (classic SPL Token, 6 decimals, the future Market PDA as mint authority, no freeze authority) and a cUSDC token account owned by the Market PDA (live markets use the PDA's associated token account). `initialize_market` creates none of these.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call initialize_market">
    The program's upgrade authority calls `initialize_market(params: InitMarketParams)`, passing the program and its `program_data` as the last two accounts, with `asset_symbol` (at most 10 bytes), `oracle_type` (`Pyth` or `Continuum`), `initial_l_price`, `initial_s_price`, `mint_fee_bps`, `redeem_fee_bps` and `keeper_authority`. With `Continuum`, the `oracle` account must be the market's `OracleConfig` PDA, which doesn't exist yet. The signer becomes `market.authority`. The market starts with `yield_enabled = true` and a yield ceiling of 0.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the oracle config">
    `initialize_oracle_config(max_confidence_interval, max_staleness, keeper_authority)`, signed by `market.authority`, creates the `OracleConfig`. `upsert_oracle_feed(priority, kind, is_active)`, signed by the config's admin, registers a Pyth price account as a feed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the market's CLP">
    `initialize_clp(oi_cap, oracle_config)`, signed by `market.authority`, creates the market's `Clp` account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create both bin books">
    `init_pool(side, step_bps, num_bins)`, signed by `GlobalClp.authority`, runs once for the long book (side 0) and once for the short book (side 1). A new book stays disabled until the keeper posts its first shape with `set_pool_shape`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the market to the keeper">
    The keeper prices only markets on its configured list, so a new market needs a keeper configuration change before it gets prices and book shapes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
