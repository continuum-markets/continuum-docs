Continuum runs on Solana **devnet**. Plans for mainnet are on the [Roadmap](/markets/roadmap).

## Cluster

```
RPC:     https://api.devnet.solana.com
WS:      wss://api.devnet.solana.com
Cluster: devnet
```

The public devnet endpoint is rate-limited. For sustained work, use a private RPC such as Helius, Triton or QuickNode.

## Faucet

The [`faucet`](/programs/faucet) program drips 1,000 cUSDC to a wallet once every 24 hours. The live `FaucetState` has `drip_amount = 1000000000` and `cooldown_seconds = 86400`.

In the app, click **Get 1000 cUSDC** on [/faucet](https://continuum.markets/faucet). From code, call the `drip` instruction; see [Faucet program](/programs/faucet). The recipient pays rent for its `DripRecord` account on the first drip, and a second drip inside 24 hours fails with `CooldownNotElapsed`.

## How prices work on devnet

### The keeper pushes every price

Every devnet market runs in the `ProxyMode` risk state, which multiplies fees and the confidence band by 1.5. In ProxyMode the programs price from `Market.user_twap_price`, which the keeper writes with mint-redeem `update_risk_state_v2`, together with `twap_updated_at`. Despite its name, `user_twap_price` is a spot price, scaled to 1e6 and adjusted for corporate actions. The oracle program's `OracleConfig` is not updated on devnet, so do not read it for live prices.

Pyth's public price endpoint now requires an API key, so the devnet keeper prices markets from a free public quote source. A GJR-GARCH volatility model sets the confidence band. Each price is checked against Hyperliquid before it is pushed, and a disagreement blocks the push. Hyperliquid can veto a price but never sets one; if it is unreachable, the price goes through unchecked. Mainnet will use Pyth (Pyth Pro).

### Committed orders on three markets

As of 10 Oct 2026, only the `Clp` accounts of QQQ, SPY and XAU have `swap_guards.enabled` set. On the other 11 markets, `commit_swap` and the instant oracle swap (`clp_swap`) revert with `SwapDisabled`, and the app hides both. The bin books (`pool_swap`) do not check that flag and trade on all 14 markets.

### No fresh price, no mint or redeem

Mint and redeem revert with `TwapNotAvailable` when `user_twap_price` is 0 or `twap_updated_at` is more than 300 s old. There is no fallback to `initial_l_price`. A keeper restart or outage longer than 300 s pauses mint and redeem in the same way.

### Off-hours

The keeper withholds a market's push once its source price is more than 120 s old. That happens when the underlying market closes: outside regular US trading hours for stocks and ETFs, and mainly at weekends for FX and metals. 300 s after the last push, mint and redeem freeze.

The bin books keep trading. The keeper replaces each book with a free-float book anchored at the last price and capped at $10,000 per side, or $1,250 per side for single-name stocks. When other venues show the price has moved, it pulls the bins on the stale side. The first fresh push reopens mint and redeem and re-centres the books.
