<Warning>
  The auction was retired on 11 October 2026 and replaced by the [order book](/flows/order-book), which matches continuously instead of every 5 minutes and supports market, limit and fill-or-kill orders. All 28 auction books were switched off with no orders open. This page describes how the program worked.
</Warning>

When the underlying market is closed, the [bin books](/flows/trade#bin-books) keep trading, but only up to a small capped size, and [committed orders](/flows/trade) can't settle because no price push arrives. The auction program covers the rest. There you trade with other traders instead of the protocol.

Every 5 minutes, each book collects the orders placed since the last match and clears them at a single price, the one at which the most quantity trades. Buyers never pay more than their limit and sellers never get less than theirs. The protocol takes no side. Both sides escrow their own cUSDC or tokens, and the program only matches them.

| | Auction |
|---|---|
| Program | `auction`, `HJ9AaPoBtGDEe7qVrdP2iakQ8qGtoEHWFK75NKpCuej5` (devnet) |
| Books | Two per market, one per leg: long tokens against cUSDC and short tokens against cUSDC |
| Matching | Every 300 s, at one clearing price per book |
| Price band | ±64 ticks around the leg's last posted NAV. The tick is 5 bps for FX (±3.2%), 12 bps for indices and gold (±7.7%), 15 bps for silver (±9.6%), 25 bps for stocks (±16%) and 40 bps for MSTR (±25.6%) |
| Fee | 10 bps of the fill, charged to each side |
| Order size | At least 25 cUSDC at the limit price |
| Order life | Up to 4 days. Unfilled quantity carries into the next match until the order fills, expires or is cancelled |

<Note>
  Auction prices never feed NAV, the oracle, γ or the CLP. A thin off-hours book is easy to push around, so nothing outside the auction program reads its prices.
</Note>

## How a match clears

Orders sit on a grid of 129 prices centred on the leg's posted NAV. A buy is placed at the highest grid price at or below its limit, a sell at the lowest at or above. When the batch ends, the program picks the clearing price in three steps:

1. The price that executes the most quantity.
2. Among those, the one where buy and sell interest are closest.
3. Among those, the middle one. This splits the gain between buyers and sellers instead of pulling the price towards a stale reference.

Every fill is at that price. If one side has more interest than the other, it is rationed by price first: the highest bids and the lowest offers fill first. Orders at the marginal price share what is left in proportion to their size. There is no time priority, so nothing is gained by racing to the start of a batch.

Rounding always favours the vault. A buyer receives the floor of their share and pays for the ceiling; a seller delivers the ceiling and is paid for the floor. Each side's shares add up to the matched quantity exactly, so a batch can never pay out more of either token than it took in. What a batch keeps (the fee and a few atoms of dust) is swept to the fee recipient when it closes.

### Orders are firm for their batch

You can't pull an order while its batch is still open. Without this rule, someone could place a large order to move the price everyone else sees, then withdraw it before the match. A cancel during a batch stops the order from carrying into the next one, and the settlement after the match refunds what is left. Once the batch has matched, a cancel refunds straight away.

The app sends an order signed in the last 45 seconds before a match to the batch after it, because a wallet prompt can outlast the batch. `place_order` accepts the current batch or the next one.

### Splits stop an order

A limit price is quoted in the market's current share units. If a split or other units change takes effect while an order is open, the order stops at its next settlement and is refunded rather than carried into a batch priced in the new units.

## Lifecycle

| Step | Instruction | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Open the batch | `open_batch` | Anyone. Idempotent; the app includes it in every order transaction |
| Place an order | `place_order` | The trader. Escrows `quantity × limit × (1 + fee)` cUSDC on a buy, `quantity` tokens on a sell |
| Match | `clear_batch` | Anyone, once the batch has ended. The keeper does it within a tick |
| Pay and carry on | `settle_order` | Anyone. Pays the fill, then carries the rest into the current batch or refunds it |
| Cancel | `settle_order` with `cancel: true` | The owner |
| Tidy up | `close_batch` | Anyone, once every order in the batch has settled. Sweeps the fee and returns the rent to whoever opened the batch |

`settle_order` never refunds a live order on a third party's say-so: if the order still has quantity, hasn't expired and the book is running, the call must pass the current batch, and the order moves into it. Only the owner's cancel, expiry, a filled order, a paused config, a disabled book, a cancelled (voided) batch or a units change ends it.

## Accounts and PDAs

All PDAs are under the auction program.

| Account | Seeds | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `AuctionConfig` | `[b"config"]` | Admin, fee recipient, pause flag |
| `Book` | `[b"book", market, [side]]` | One per leg; `side` is 0 for long, 1 for short. Holds the grid, fee, batch length, limits and the last match |
| Book vaults | the book PDA's associated token accounts | One for the leg's token, one for cUSDC. Shared by every batch of the book |
| `Batch` | `[b"batch", book, batch_id as u64 LE]` | `batch_id = unix_timestamp / batch_secs`. Per-tick totals on both sides, the clearing result and a ledger of what the batch took in and paid out |
| `Order` | `[b"order", book, owner, nonce as u64 LE]` | The limit, quantity left, escrow, the batch it is in and its fills so far |

`Batch` keeps its scalar fields first, so `book` sits at byte 8 and `status` at byte 76 (0 open, 1 cleared, 2 voided). `Order.owner` sits at byte 8, which lets a wallet find its orders with one `getProgramAccounts` filter.

## Place an order

```typescript
import { BN } from "@coral-xyz/anchor";
import { PublicKey, SystemProgram } from "@solana/web3.js";
import { getAssociatedTokenAddressSync, TOKEN_PROGRAM_ID } from "@solana/spl-token";

const AUCTION = new PublicKey("HJ9AaPoBtGDEe7qVrdP2iakQ8qGtoEHWFK75NKpCuej5");
const pda = (...seeds: Buffer[]) => PublicKey.findProgramAddressSync(seeds, AUCTION)[0];
const u64 = (v: bigint) => { const b = Buffer.alloc(8); b.writeBigUInt64LE(v); return b; };

// `program` is an Anchor Program built from the auction IDL; `market` is the
// mint-redeem Market PDA; `usdc` the market's collateral mint.
const side = 0; // long
const book = pda(Buffer.from("book"), market.toBuffer(), Buffer.from([side]));
const { batchSecs } = await program.account.book.fetch(book);
const batchId = BigInt(Math.floor(Date.now() / 1000 / batchSecs));
const batch = pda(Buffer.from("batch"), book.toBuffer(), u64(batchId));
const nonce = BigInt(Date.now());
const order = pda(Buffer.from("order"), book.toBuffer(), wallet.publicKey.toBuffer(), u64(nonce));

await program.methods.openBatch({ batchId: new BN(batchId.toString()) })
  .accounts({ payer: wallet.publicKey, book, market })
  .postInstructions([
    await program.methods.placeOrder({
      nonce: new BN(nonce.toString()),
      isBuy: true,
      limitPrice: new BN(4_210_000_000),  // $4,210.00 a token, 6 decimals
      quantity: new BN(2_000_000),        // 2 tokens, 6 decimals
      expiresAt: new BN(Math.floor(Date.now() / 1000) + 86_400),
    }).accounts({
      owner: wallet.publicKey, book, market, batch, order,
      ownerIn: getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(usdc, wallet.publicKey),
      vaultIn: getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(usdc, book, true),
    }).instruction(),
  ])
  .rpc();
```

Create both of your associated token accounts (cUSDC and the leg's token) first: settlement pays into them.

## Errors

| Code | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 6002 | `Paused` | New orders are paused |
| 6003 | `BookDisabled` | The book isn't taking orders |
| 6009 | `WrongBatch` | `open_batch` was given a batch other than the current or next one |
| 6010 | `BatchNotOpen` | The batch has ended, cleared or been voided |
| 6011 | `BatchStillOpen` | `clear_batch` before the batch ended |
| 6012 | `BatchNotCleared` | `settle_order` before the batch matched |
| 6017 | `BelowMinimum` | The order is under the minimum size at its limit |
| 6018 | `InvalidExpiry` | Expiry in the past or beyond the book's maximum |
| 6019 | `LimitOutsideBand` | A bid below the band or an offer above it would never trade |
| 6021 | `NextBatchRequired` | A live order can only be carried forward, so pass the current batch |

The full list is in the [IDL](https://github.com/continuum-markets/continuum-docs/tree/main/idl).
