---
title: Redeem paired tokens
description: Burn long and short tokens together and receive cUSDC at worst-case quotes.
---

`redeem_paired(l_amount, s_amount)` burns L and S from the user and pays their value in cUSDC from the market's collateral vault. Both amounts must be above zero. They don't have to match in quantity or value, but together they must be worth at least 10 cUSDC at the redeem quotes.

There is no single-leg redeem. With either amount at 0 the program fails with `InvalidAmount`. To get out of a leg you hold on its own, [sell it](#exiting-one-leg).

## Math

```
L_quote  = P - c
S_quote  = K / (P + c) * γ
gross    = l_amount * L_quote + s_amount * S_quote     // must be at least 10 cUSDC
payout   = min(gross, gross * total_collateral / total_claims)

fee_bps  = redeem_fee_bps * state_mult / 100
fee      = payout * fee_bps / 10_000
return   = payout - fee
```

`P`, `c`, `K`, γ and `state_mult` mean the same as on [Mint](/flows/mint#math): the spot price, the confidence band, the constant product, the short-leg decay and the risk-state multiplier (1.5× in `ProxyMode`). The quotes run against the user again: L is priced below its NAV at `P - c`, and S below its NAV at `K / (P + c) * γ`. Every division rounds down.

`total_claims` values every outstanding L and S at the same quotes. While the vault holds at least that much, `payout` equals `gross`. If it holds less, every redeemer gets the same pro-rata share (see [below](#under-collateralization-pro-rata-redemption)). The fee is charged on `payout`.

The fee is the same posted value as the mint fee: QQQ posted 18 bps on 10 Oct 2026 and charged 27 bps in `ProxyMode`. A tenth of the fee (`dev_tax`) goes to the fee recipient, and the rest stays in the vault. See [Mint fees](/flows/mint#fees).

A worked example with QQQ's last push before the weekend, at 20:15 UTC on 9 Oct 2026: L's NAV was 751.27 cUSDC and S's was 304.599737, with γ at 0.99321461. QQQ quotes at NAV because its worst-case quoting is off. At that price, redeeming 0.663742 QQQL with 1.637066 QQQS gives a gross of 997.299325 cUSDC, a 27 bps fee of 2.692708 and a return of 994.606617 cUSDC.

## Price freshness

Redeem has the same gate as mint. Outside `Normal` it reverts with `TwapNotAvailable` when the price is 0 or more than 300 seconds old. While the underlying market is closed the keeper normally withholds pushes, so redeem pauses until trading resumes. The [bin books](/flows/trade) keep quoting off-hours if you need to exit then.

## Accounts

Redeem takes the mint accounts, with the same names and checks, plus one ignored slot at the end.

| Account | Notes |
|---|---|
| `market` (mut) | Market PDA |
| `user` (signer, mut) | Owns the L and S being burned |
| `userCollateral` (mut) | Receives the cUSDC |
| `userLongAccount`, `userShortAccount` (mut) | The accounts the burns come from |
| `longMint`, `shortMint` (mut) | From the market |
| `collateralVault` (mut) | Pays out the cUSDC |
| `devTokenAccount` (mut) | The fee recipient's cUSDC account; receives `dev_tax` |
| `oracle` | `market.oracle_address` |
| `tokenProgram` | SPL Token |
| `collateralAuthority` (optional) | Ignored. The program never reads this slot and accepts any account. Pass `null`, and Anchor sends mint-redeem's program ID |

A redemption whose L and S values differ by more than \$1 also passes five remaining accounts after these, for the [exposure cap](#exposure-cap).

Every market's vault is owned by its Market PDA, which signs the payout. The slot is left over from the unified vault, which the upgrade of 11 Oct 2026 removed. The IDL published that day gives the slot no seeds, so Anchor can't fill it in for you. Pass `collateralAuthority: null`, as below.

## TypeScript

Set up `program`, `user`, `marketPda`, `market` and the four token accounts as on [Mint](/flows/mint#typescript), then:

```typescript
await program.methods
  .redeemPaired(new BN(663_742), new BN(1_637_066)) // 0.663742 QQQL and 1.637066 QQQS
  .accounts({
    market: marketPda,
    user,
    userCollateral,
    userLongAccount,
    userShortAccount,
    longMint: market.longMint,
    shortMint: market.shortMint,
    collateralVault: market.collateralVault,
    devTokenAccount,
    oracle: market.oracleAddress,
    tokenProgram: TOKEN_PROGRAM_ID,
    collateralAuthority: null, // ignored slot
  })
  .rpc();
```

## Exiting one leg

If you minted and then sold one side, you hold the other side alone, and the program won't redeem it on its own. Sell it instead:

- With a [committed order](/flows/trade#committed-orders) while the market is open. It fills at NAV minus the market's commit spread, at a keeper price push after you commit.
- On its [bin book](/flows/trade#bin-books) with `pool_swap` while the market's price is stale. The fill is instant, at the book's prices, with slippage that grows with size.

If you hold both legs, in any ratio, one `redeem_paired` can burn all of them. Your profit or loss is all the cUSDC you received, from redeems and sales, minus what you paid to mint.

For a pair, compare the redeem fee with the cost of selling. QQQ's redeem fee was 27 bps as of 10 Oct 2026. A committed sale pays the commit spread, 18 bps on QQQ that day, one leg at a time. The bin books trade only while mint and redeem are frozen; there the first bins of a QQQ book sit one 20 bps core step from the book's centre, which the keeper shifts with the CLP's inventory, and larger sales walk into wider bins.

## Limits

There is no fixed size cap, but a redemption whose L and S values differ by more than \$1 is held to the market's [exposure cap](#exposure-cap). A redeem can't burn more than the market's outstanding supply of either leg (`InvalidAmount`). The payout plus `dev_tax` must fit within the market's liquid collateral, `total_collateral` minus `deployed_to_yield`, and within the vault's token balance (`InsufficientLiquidCollateral`). When it doesn't, [queue a redemption request](#when-the-vault-cannot-pay-yet) instead. The program doesn't check your balances; burning more than you hold fails inside SPL Token.

## Exposure cap

Redeeming equal values of L and S leaves what traders hold net unchanged. Redeeming unequal values doesn't. Mint a pair and redeem only the S, say, and traders end up net long without ever touching the CLP. So a redemption whose L and S values differ by more than \$1, at the redeem NAVs, is checked against the market's exposure cap before anything is burned. It is treated as a trade: redeeming more S than L raises traders' net long position, and redeeming more L than S lowers it. The rule is the one in [Trade](/flows/trade#exposure-cap): it passes if it leaves the net position inside the cap, or moves it toward zero. The sizing and the reasoning are in [Solvency](/concepts/solvency#6-exposure-cap).

A lopsided redemption must pass five remaining accounts, in this order:

1. The market's `Clp` account (PDA `[b"clp", market]` under clp).
2. The pool long synth vault.
3. The pool short synth vault.
4. The CLP's long token account.
5. The CLP's short token account.

Without them it fails with `ExposureAccountsMissing` (6062). Each account is checked against its derived address, owner and mint (`ExposureAccountMismatch`, 6061), so an empty look-alike can't understate the CLP's holdings. A redemption past the cap fails with `ExposureCapExceeded` (6060). A redemption within \$1 of balanced skips the check and needs no extra accounts. A lopsided one needs them even on a market whose `oi_cap` is 0, where the check then passes. The market's CLP redeeming its own inventory (`vault_redeem_pairs`) is exempt, because burning CLP-held tokens lowers a supply and the CLP's holding of it together, so traders' net position doesn't move. `redeem_paired_with_waiver` and `request_redemption` follow the same rule, and a request is checked when it is made. If you hit the cap, redeem more evenly, sell the extra leg on a venue with room, or wait for the room to grow.

## When the vault cannot pay yet

`redeem_paired` pays only from cash in the vault. On mainnet the keeper lends most of a market's collateral and keeps a liquid share in the vault, so a large redeem can ask for more than the vault holds right now. It then reverts with `InsufficientLiquidCollateral` (6022). Nothing is lost; the redemption has to go through a request instead:

1. **Request.** `request_redemption(l_amount, s_amount)` moves your L and S into escrow accounts owned by a `RedemptionRequest` PDA. It runs the same checks as a redeem (market active, not in `Stress`, a price at most 300 s old, at least 10 cUSDC) but burns and pays nothing.
2. **Settle.** The keeper's redemption settler pays the largest part of the request that the vault's cash covers, using `settle_redemption`. If more is needed it recalls the market's lending deposits in the same transaction, and for what the venues can't release in time it takes a loan from the treasury (`advance_to_market`) within the market's advance cap. Each part is priced at the redeem NAVs and fee at the moment it settles, with the same pro-rata haircut as `redeem_paired`, and burned from escrow. A request can therefore fill in several parts.
3. **Cancel.** `cancel_redemption` returns whatever is still unpaid and closes the request, at any time. It needs no keeper and no fresh price.

The settler is a keeper task that runs only when `REDEMPTION_SETTLE_ENABLED` is true (default false; see [Running a keeper](/keeper/running)). While it is off, a request waits until you cancel it. The accounts are in [mint-redeem → RedemptionRequest](/programs/mint-redeem#redemptionrequest).

## Under-collateralization: pro-rata redemption

The vault is meant to stay over-collateralized. In the tail, a large move on a lopsided book can still push collateral below the value of all outstanding tokens. Redeem keeps paying in that case, but pro-rata: the `payout` line in [Math](#math) multiplies `gross` by `min(1, CR)`, where `CR = total_collateral / total_claims`.

A redeem can never pay out more than the vault holds, because `gross` can't exceed `total_claims`. Every redeemer gets the same `min(1, CR)` factor, and the ratio stays the same for everyone left, so redeeming early gains nothing. While `CR` is at or above 100% the factor is 1 and you receive the full quote.

## Behavior across risk states

| Risk state | Price | Fee and confidence multiplier | Redeem |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Normal` | The market's oracle account | 1× | Allowed |
| `ProxyMode` (every devnet market) | `user_twap_price`, at most 300 s old | 1.5× | Allowed |
| `Stress` | `user_twap_price`, at most 300 s old | 3× | Blocked by default |
| `Recovery` | `user_twap_price`, at most 300 s old | Falls linearly from 3× to 1× over the hour after `risk_state_updated_at` | Allowed |

`Stress` blocks redeem unless the market's `allow_redeem_in_stress` is true, and it is false on every live market. The `Recovery` multiplier behaves as described on [Mint](/flows/mint#behavior-across-risk-states).

## Errors

| Error | Code | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `InvalidAmount` | 6001 | `l_amount` or `s_amount` is 0, an amount exceeds that leg's outstanding supply, or an account doesn't match the market |
| `BelowMinimum` | 6019 | The redeem is worth less than 10 cUSDC at the quotes |
| `TwapNotAvailable` | 6012 | The price is 0 or more than 300 s old. Usually the underlying market is closed |
| `RedeemNotAllowedInStress` | 6010 | The market is in `Stress` |
| `InsufficientLiquidCollateral` | 6022 | The payout plus `dev_tax` exceeds the market's liquid collateral or the vault balance. Queue a [redemption request](#when-the-vault-cannot-pay-yet), or retry after the keeper recalls from lending |
| `MarketNotActive` | 6000 | `market.is_active` is false |
| `OraclePriceUnavailable` | 6005 | A worst-case quote came out as 0 |
| `Unauthorized` | 6021 | `devTokenAccount` isn't owned by `market.fee_recipient` |
| `ExposureCapExceeded` | 6060 | A redemption more than \$1 lopsided would push the market's net exposure past its cap |
| `ExposureAccountMismatch` | 6061 | An exposure account isn't the market's `Clp`, pool synth vault or CLP token account |
| `ExposureAccountsMissing` | 6062 | A redemption more than \$1 lopsided without the five exposure accounts |
| SPL Token error `0x1` | | Your L or S balance is below the amount |

See the [full error catalog](/reference/errors).

## Fee waivers

`redeem_paired_with_waiver` takes the same accounts plus the user's `FeeWaiver` PDA and skips the fee under the conditions on [Mint](/flows/mint#fee-waivers).

## What you receive

```
dev_tax = fee * dev_tax_pct / 100              // dev_tax_pct = 10

user cUSDC account        +return
collateral vault          -(return + dev_tax)
fee recipient             +dev_tax
user L account            -l_amount (burned)
user S account            -s_amount (burned)

market.total_l_supply    -= l_amount
market.total_s_supply    -= s_amount
market.total_collateral  -= return + dev_tax
```

The rest of the fee never leaves the vault. The program then emits `PairedActionExecuted` with `action` 1, `gross_collateral_amount` (the payout), `collateral_amount` (the return), `fee_amount` and `dev_tax`.
