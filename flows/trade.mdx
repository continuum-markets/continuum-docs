---
title: Trade
description: Buy and sell long or short tokens with committed orders while a market is open, and on the bin books while its price is stale.
---

Continuum trades L and S tokens on its own venues in the CLP program. Which one fills depends on whether the market's price is live:

| Venue | Instruction | When it fills | Price | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Committed order | `commit_swap`, then `settle_swap` | While the keeper pushes prices, at the first push after the commit | NAV ± `max(commit_spread_bps, 10)` bps | No cap; bounded by CLP inventory and the collateral floor |
| Bin book | `pool_swap` | Only while the price is stale: the risk state isn't `Normal` and the last push is more than 300 s old | Walks the book's bins, with real slippage | The standing depth, small by design |

Since 11 Oct 2026, open-hours trading is committed orders only. `pool_swap` fails with `PoolClosedWhileLive` whenever the market's price is live. The CLP's instant oracle swap, `clp_swap`, is [retired](#retired-instant-oracle-swap): the instruction keeps its accounts and arguments, but every call fails with `SwapDisabled`. Committed orders work on every market except MSTR, whose swap guards are off.

The app places every trade from your [trading account](/flows/trading-account). While the market is open it uses committed orders, at any size, so every in-hours fill is priced by a price push that happens after you commit. While the market is closed or its price updates are paused, no price push would arrive to settle a committed order, so the app shows the bin book's quotes, which fill instantly up to its capped size, and traders' orders on the [order book](/flows/order-book), where size is limited only by who is on the other side, as one book. A market order takes the cheapest offers first when you buy and the highest bids first when you sell, from either, in one transaction. When the keeper has pulled one side of the bin book because outside markets moved, the app warns anyone about to trade into the side that still prices the close.

<Note>
  Mint and redeem open and close a *paired* position (see [Mint](/flows/mint) and [Redeem](/flows/redeem)). The venues on this page buy or sell one leg.
</Note>

## Bin books

`pool_swap` trades against a native bin book. Each market has two, one per leg: QQQ's long book and QQQ's short book are separate `Pool` accounts. A book lives inline in its `Pool` account, with up to 16 bins and no rent per bin. `asks[i]` holds tokens of the leg for sale above the centre price `base_price_1e6`, and `bids[i]` holds cUSDC below it.

The keeper sets the centre from NAV, shifted by the CLP's inventory, and reshapes the book as the price moves. Off-hours it posts a capped free-float book, and a guard lets it pull bins only on the stale side. See [Book management](/keeper/book-management).

Bins use two step sizes: a fine core step (`step_bps`) for the first `core_bins` bins on each side, then a wider wing step (`wing_step_bps`). Ask bin `i`, counting from 0, sits `i + 1` steps above the centre and bid bin `i` sits `i + 1` steps below it. Each step up multiplies the price by `1 + step / 10_000`, and each step down divides by it. On 10 Oct 2026 each QQQ book had 12 bins: 3 core bins 20 bps apart, then 300 bps wing steps. A swap walks the bins outward from the centre. If the book runs out, the swap fills what it can and charges only for that, and `min_out` still applies.

`pool_swap` charges no swap fee; you pay the distance from the centre. It fills only while the market floats: the market's risk state must not be `Normal`, and its last price push must be more than 300 s old, the same age at which mint and redeem freeze. In any other case it fails with `PoolClosedWhileLive`, and you trade with a committed order instead. Every devnet market runs in `ProxyMode`, so on devnet a book opens 300 s after the last push and closes again at the next one.

There is no pool-level kill switch. A new pool stays disabled until the keeper posts its first shape, and after that nothing disables or freezes it: no instruction sets `pool.frozen`, and `set_pool_shape` always re-enables the pool. Freezing the CLP's swap guards stops committed orders but not `pool_swap`. The keeper can still empty a book by posting all-zero bins, and the [rolling-window cap](#rolling-window-cap) can pause it.

### Accounts

`pool_swap` takes `PoolSwapParams { direction, amount_in, min_out }`. `direction` is 0 to buy the leg with cUSDC and 1 to sell it for cUSDC. `amount_in` is cUSDC on a buy and tokens of the leg on a sell, both with 6 decimals.

| Account | Notes |
|---|---|
| `user` (signer) | The trader |
| `userUsdc` (mut) | The trader's cUSDC account |
| `userSynth` (mut) | The trader's token account for the book's leg. Must exist |
| `pool` (mut) | Pool PDA, seeds `[b"pool", market, [side]]` under clp. `side` is 0 for long, 1 for short |
| `clp` (mut) | The market's CLP PDA, seeds `[b"clp", market]`. Holds the swap guards and the rolling-window counter |
| `synthVault` (mut) | The pool's token account for the leg (its ATA) |
| `usdcVault` (mut) | The pool's cUSDC account (its ATA) |
| `tokenProgram` | SPL Token |
| `revenueAttribution` (mut) | PDA `[b"revenue_attribution"]` under clp (`A8mK2pnagabEcnkSiWieEZyQjjNWFYm1Z91dUVMXe6a4`) |
| `market` | The book's Market PDA under mint_redeem, read for its risk state and price age. Must come last |

### Setup

Both samples on this page start from this setup.

```typescript
import { Program, BN } from "@coral-xyz/anchor";
import { PublicKey, SystemProgram } from "@solana/web3.js";
import {
  getAssociatedTokenAddressSync,
  createAssociatedTokenAccountIdempotentInstruction,
  TOKEN_PROGRAM_ID,
} from "@solana/spl-token";
import mintRedeemIdl from "./mint_redeem.json"; // idl/ in the docs repo
import clpIdl from "./clp.json";

const mintRedeem = new Program(mintRedeemIdl, provider); // provider: an AnchorProvider for the trader's wallet
const clp = new Program(clpIdl, provider);
const user = provider.wallet.publicKey;

const [marketPda] = PublicKey.findProgramAddressSync(
  [Buffer.from("market"), Buffer.from("QQQ")],
  mintRedeem.programId,
);
const market = await mintRedeem.account.market.fetch(marketPda);
const [clpPda] = PublicKey.findProgramAddressSync(
  [Buffer.from("clp"), marketPda.toBuffer()],
  clp.programId,
);
```

### Quote and swap

```typescript
// Mirrors the program's bin walk, so `out` matches the fill exactly.
function quotePoolSwap(pool: any, direction: 0 | 1, amountIn: bigint) {
  const bins = direction === 0 ? pool.asks : pool.bids;
  let price = BigInt(pool.basePrice1E6.toString());
  let out = 0n;
  let left = amountIn;
  for (let i = 0; i < pool.numBins && left > 0n; i++) {
    const step = BigInt(i < pool.coreBins ? pool.stepBps : pool.wingStepBps || pool.stepBps);
    price = direction === 0
      ? (price * (10_000n + step)) / 10_000n  // ask bin i: i + 1 steps above the centre
      : (price * 10_000n) / (10_000n + step); // bid bin i: i + 1 steps below it
    const reserve = BigInt(bins[i].toString());
    if (reserve === 0n) continue;
    const full = direction === 0
      ? (reserve * price) / 1_000_000n  // cUSDC that empties this ask bin
      : (reserve * 1_000_000n) / price; // tokens that empty this bid bin
    if (left >= full && full > 0n) {
      out += reserve;
      left -= full;
      continue;
    }
    const part = direction === 0 ? (left * 1_000_000n) / price : (left * price) / 1_000_000n;
    out += part < reserve ? part : reserve;
    left = 0n;
  }
  return { out, spent: amountIn - left }; // spent < amountIn when the book runs out
}

const side = 0;       // 0 = long leg, 1 = short leg
const direction = 0;  // 0 = buy with cUSDC, 1 = sell for cUSDC
const amountIn = 100_000_000n; // 100 cUSDC

const [poolPda] = PublicKey.findProgramAddressSync(
  [Buffer.from("pool"), marketPda.toBuffer(), Buffer.from([side])],
  clp.programId,
);
const [revenueAttribution] = PublicKey.findProgramAddressSync(
  [Buffer.from("revenue_attribution")],
  clp.programId,
);

const pool = await clp.account.pool.fetch(poolPda);
const quote = quotePoolSwap(pool, direction, amountIn);
const minOut = (quote.out * 9_950n) / 10_000n; // 0.5% slippage tolerance
const userSynth = getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(pool.synthMint, user);

await clp.methods
  .poolSwap({ direction, amountIn: new BN(amountIn.toString()), minOut: new BN(minOut.toString()) })
  .accounts({
    user,
    userUsdc: getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(pool.usdcMint, user),
    userSynth,
    pool: poolPda,
    clp: clpPda,
    synthVault: getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(pool.synthMint, poolPda, true),
    usdcVault: getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(pool.usdcMint, poolPda, true),
    tokenProgram: TOKEN_PROGRAM_ID,
    revenueAttribution,
    market: marketPda, // the mint-redeem Market, last
  })
  .preInstructions([
    createAssociatedTokenAccountIdempotentInstruction(user, userSynth, user, pool.synthMint),
  ])
  .rpc();
```

Against an unchanged book, `quote.out` is exactly what the swap returns. On Saturday 10 Oct 2026, with the market closed, it quoted 0.132577 QQQL for 100 cUSDC on the QQQ long book, and a simulated `pool_swap` returned the same.

Standing depth is small on purpose. It is sized to demand rather than TVL, because the losses to informed traders grow with the depth deployed. On Saturday 10 Oct 2026 the QQQ long book held 5,000 cUSDC of bids and 10.13 QQQL (about 7,600 cUSDC) of asks. The single-stock books held 1,000 to 1,250 cUSDC a side, their off-hours cap. Larger orders belong in a committed order once the market opens, or on the [order book](/flows/order-book).

## Rolling-window cap

`pool_swap` is held to one notional cap per market, kept in the market's `Clp` account. Each fill adds its cUSDC amount to `swap_window_used`, and a fill that would take it past `window_cap_usd` reverts with `WindowCapExceeded`. The count resets once `window_secs` have passed since the window opened. As of 11 Oct 2026, QQQ, SPY and XAU are capped at 1,000,000 cUSDC per 3,600 s. The other 11 markets have no cap set, and 0 means uncapped. Committed orders don't count toward the cap.

## Committed orders

You put your input in escrow under an order account, and the order fills at a keeper price push after your commit, at NAV ± the commit spread:

```
spread = max(commit_spread_bps, 10)         // per market, in the Clp account
buy:   out = amount_in / (NAV * (1 + spread / 10_000))
sell:  out = amount_in * NAV * (1 - spread / 10_000)
```

NAV is the leg's NAV at the settling push: `P` for the long leg and `K / P * γ` for the short leg (see [Mint](/flows/mint#math)). The keeper posts `commit_spread_bps` as `clamp(half-spread, 10, 500)` from its volatility model. As of 10 Oct 2026 it was 18 bps on QQQ, 15 on SPY and 18 on XAU.

The price is set after you commit, so nobody can trade against a stale quote. That is why committed orders have no size cap and skip the rolling window. They are bounded by the price checks at settle, the collateral floor and the CLP's inventory.

Committed orders need the market's swap guards (`Clp.swap_guards`, set with `configure_swap`) to be enabled and not frozen. Since 11 Oct 2026 they are on for 13 markets, every market except MSTR, with the same settings:

| Guard | Setting | Effect at settle |
|---|---|---|
| `max_staleness_secs` | 600 | The price must be at most 600 s old |
| `max_confidence_bps` | 5,000 | The confidence band must be at most half the price, so in practice this check is off |
| `max_deviation_bps` | 10,000 | Off. The price write itself refuses a move of more than 25% per push |
| `collateral_floor_bps` | 10,000 | The market must be at least 100% collateralized at the settle price |

Their spread (`spread_bps`) and window cap were left unchanged. `spread_bps` priced the retired `clp_swap`, and no live instruction reads it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Commit">
    `commit_swap(side, direction, amount_in, min_out, nonce)` moves your input (cUSDC on a buy, tokens of the leg on a sell) into an escrow token account owned by the order PDA. It needs the market active (`MarketInactive`) and its swap guards enabled (`SwapDisabled`) and not frozen (`SwapFrozen`). Your payout account must already exist. The program checks it at commit, so settlement can't fail on a missing account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle (permissionless)">
    `settle_swap` becomes valid once `market.twap_updated_at` is later than the commit, in a later second. It stays valid until `expires_at`. Anyone can call it, and the keeper sends it after the first push that qualifies. It fills at the market's current price, which may come from a later push than the first one after your commit.

    - If the output misses `min_out`, the order is refunded in full and closed.
    - If the market is below its collateral floor (a 100% collateral ratio at the settle price), or the price fails the checks above, settle reverts. The order stays pending and fills at a later push, if one passes before it expires.
    - If the CLP lacks inventory, settle reverts and the order stays pending while the keeper provisions it.

    While the underlying market is closed the keeper normally withholds pushes, so an order committed then can't settle and expires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refund (after the TTL)">
    An order expires 180 s after the commit. After that, anyone can send `cancel_swap`. It returns the escrow to the owner's token account and the rent to the owner, and needs neither of their signatures. Before expiry nobody can cancel (`OrderNotExpired`). Cancel checks nothing else (no price, keeper, freeze or market state), so escrowed funds can always be recovered after expiry. The keeper refunds every expired order about 5 s after expiry, and the app shows a **Refund now** button if an order is still open 60 s after it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Settling, refunding and cancelling all close the order and escrow accounts and return their rent to you.

<Note>
  On devnet the fill price (`market.user_twap_price`) and the freshness check (`market.twap_updated_at`) both come from keeper pushes, so a committed order trusts the keeper's price.
</Note>

### Order account

PDA seeds `[b"order", user, market, nonce]` under clp, with `nonce` as 8 little-endian bytes. `nonce` is any u64 you pick; a fresh one per order lets you keep several open at once. Keep it, or the order address, so you can find the order later (see [Reading state](/flows/read-state#pending-committed-orders)).

```rust
pub struct Order {
    pub user: Pubkey,      // offset 8 (after the discriminator)
    pub market: Pubkey,    // offset 40
    pub side: u8,          // 0 = long, 1 = short
    pub direction: u8,     // 0 = buy, 1 = sell
    pub status: u8,        // offset 74; 0 = pending, the only stored state
    pub bump: u8,
    pub escrow_bump: u8,
    pub amount_in: u64,
    pub min_out: u64,      // a miss refunds in full
    pub created_at: i64,   // settle needs twap_updated_at > created_at
    pub expires_at: i64,   // created_at + 180; settle up to it, cancel after it
    pub nonce: u64,
}
```

### Accounts: `commit_swap`

| Account | Notes |
|---|---|
| `user` (signer, mut) | Pays rent for the order and escrow, which comes back when the order closes |
| `market` | Market PDA under mint_redeem. Fixes the leg mints |
| `clp` | CLP PDA, seeds `[b"clp", market]` |
| `order` (init) | Order PDA, as above |
| `inMint` | Mint of the input: cUSDC on a buy, the leg's mint on a sell |
| `escrow` (init) | Token account, seeds `[b"escrow", order]`, owned by the order PDA |
| `userIn` (mut) | Your input token account |
| `userOut` | Your payout token account. Must exist at commit |
| `clpVault` | The CLP's cUSDC account (ATA of the CLP PDA) |
| `tokenProgram`, `systemProgram` | |

### Accounts: `settle_swap` and `cancel_swap`

`settle_swap` takes `cranker` (signer: anyone, who pays the transaction fee and gets nothing), `user` (`order.user`, who receives the rent), `order`, `escrow`, `clp`, `clpVault` and `clpSynth` (the CLP's cUSDC and leg inventory), `market`, `userOut`, `userIn` and `tokenProgram`. All but `cranker`, `market` and `tokenProgram` are mutable.

`cancel_swap` takes `user` (the order's owner, who receives the rent; not a signer), `order`, `escrow`, `userIn` (the owner's token account for the escrowed mint, which receives the refund) and `tokenProgram`. All but `tokenProgram` are mutable. Whoever sends the transaction pays its fee.

### Commit and cancel

This sample buys QQQ's long leg with 10,000 cUSDC, starting from the [setup](#setup) above. It works on every market except MSTR, while the market's price is live.

```typescript
const orderAmount = 10_000_000_000n; // 10,000 cUSDC
const nonce = new BN(Date.now());    // any u64; keep it to find the order later

const [orderPda] = PublicKey.findProgramAddressSync(
  [Buffer.from("order"), user.toBuffer(), marketPda.toBuffer(), nonce.toArrayLike(Buffer, "le", 8)],
  clp.programId,
);
const [escrowPda] = PublicKey.findProgramAddressSync(
  [Buffer.from("escrow"), orderPda.toBuffer()],
  clp.programId,
);

// Estimate the fill at the current price. settle_swap reprices at the settling push.
const clpState = await clp.account.clp.fetch(clpPda);
const spreadBps = BigInt(Math.max(clpState.commitSpreadBps, 10));
const nav = BigInt(market.userTwapPrice.toString()); // long-leg NAV, 1e6
const price = (nav * (10_000n + spreadBps)) / 10_000n; // a buy pays NAV * (1 + spread)
const orderMinOut = (((orderAmount * 1_000_000n) / price) * 99n) / 100n; // 1% tolerance

const userIn = getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(market.collateralMint, user);
const userOut = getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(market.longMint, user); // must exist before the commit

await clp.methods
  .commitSwap(0 /* long */, 0 /* buy */, new BN(orderAmount.toString()), new BN(orderMinOut.toString()), nonce)
  .accounts({
    user,
    market: marketPda,
    clp: clpPda,
    order: orderPda,
    inMint: market.collateralMint, // the leg's mint on a sell
    escrow: escrowPda,
    userIn,
    userOut,
    clpVault: getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(market.collateralMint, clpPda, true),
    tokenProgram: TOKEN_PROGRAM_ID,
    systemProgram: SystemProgram.programId,
  })
  .rpc();

// Wait for the keeper's settle: watch userOut or the OrderSettled event.
// The keeper refunds an order about 5 s after expiresAt (commit time + 180 s).
// If it is still open after that, anyone can send the refund; it goes to the owner:
await clp.methods
  .cancelSwap()
  .accounts({ user, order: orderPda, escrow: escrowPda, userIn, tokenProgram: TOKEN_PROGRAM_ID })
  .rpc();
```

The order emits `OrderCommitted`, then one of `OrderSettled` (with `out`, `nav` and `base_price`), `OrderRefunded` (a `min_out` miss) or `OrderCancelled`. Fills also emit `SwapExecuted`, whose `route` is 1 for `pool_swap` and 2 for a committed settlement. Route 0 belonged to the retired `clp_swap`.

## Retired: instant oracle swap

`clp_swap(side, direction, amount_in, min_out)` filled instantly from CLP inventory at NAV ± `spread_bps`, with no book and no slippage, on QQQ, SPY and XAU. It filled at the last pushed price, which is a stale quote between pushes, and it was retired on 11 Oct 2026. The instruction keeps its accounts and arguments, so existing clients still decode it, but every call fails with `SwapDisabled`. Use a committed order instead.

## Errors

| Error | Code | Where | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SwapDisabled` | 6003 | `pool_swap` | The keeper hasn't posted the pool's first shape yet |
| `SwapDisabled` | 6003 | `commit_swap` | The market's CLP swap guards aren't enabled (MSTR as of 11 Oct 2026) |
| `SwapDisabled` | 6003 | `clp_swap` | Always: the instant swap is retired |
| `SwapFrozen` | 6004 | `commit_swap`, `settle_swap` | The CLP's swap guards are frozen |
| `SwapAccountMismatch` | 6006 | all | An account has the wrong mint, owner or address |
| `SlippageExceeded` | 6008 | `pool_swap` | The output is below `min_out` |
| `OrderExpired` | 6009 | `settle_swap` | Past `expires_at`; the order is refunded instead |
| `OrderNotExpired` | 6010 | `cancel_swap` | Not yet past `expires_at` |
| `NoFreshOraclePush` | 6011 | `settle_swap` | No price push since the commit |
| `OracleStale`, `OracleConfidenceTooWide`, `OracleDeviationTooLarge` | 6012 to 6014 | `settle_swap` | A price check failed; the order waits for a later push |
| `WindowCapExceeded` | 6015 | `pool_swap` | The rolling-window cap is used up |
| `CollateralFloorBreach` | 6016 | `settle_swap` | The market is below its collateral floor; the order waits for a later push |
| `InsufficientFunds` | 6018 | `pool_swap`, `settle_swap` | The book has nothing on that side, or the CLP lacks the inventory |
| `PoolClosedWhileLive` | 6044 | `pool_swap` | The market's price is live; use a committed order |
