## A to C

**Anchor** - The Solana smart-contract framework Continuum is built on. Version 0.32.1.

**Ask trim** - Keeper inventory rule: ask-side synth worth more than 1.5× the bid side sweeps back to CLP custody (`pool_sweep_synth`). Ask depth the bid side cannot absorb only invites arbitrage.

**`asset_symbol`** - The market's ticker, for example `"QQQ"`. Up to 10 characters. It seeds the Market PDA.

**ATA** (Associated Token Account) - Solana's standard derivation for a wallet's token account: `getAssociatedTokenAddressSync(mint, owner)`. Always derived, not always created, so create it idempotently before instructions that expect it.

**Authority** - A signer with rights over an account: `market.authority` (the market admin), `market.keeper_authority`, `oracle_config.admin_authority`, `oracle_config.keeper_authority`, the CLP authority on `GlobalClp` and each `Clp`, and the treasury guardian on `TreasuryPolicy`.

**Base price** - A book's posted centre, `Pool.base_price_1e6`: NAV moved by the inventory centre-shift, or the last price during free float. Ask bins ladder above it, bid bins below.

**Bid cap** - Keeper inventory rule: standing bid cUSDC is held at $5,000 per side (`CLMM_BID_TARGET_USD`). The excess goes to the CLP vault, and bids that sellers spent are refilled from it. Large sells use committed orders on the markets that have them.

**Bin step** - The spacing between bins, in bps. Books use a fine core step for the first `core_bins` bins and a wide wing step beyond. On the live books the core is 10 to 50 bps over 3 bins and the wings 200 to 700 bps.

**bps** (basis points) - 1 bps = 0.01%. Fees are quoted in bps: `mint_fee_bps = 18` means 0.18%.

**CLP** (Continuous Liquidity Provider) - Continuum's on-chain treasury and trading program. It holds protocol capital and hosts the bin books, committed orders and the instant oracle swap. → [CLP concept](/concepts/clp).

**CNTM** - Continuum's planned governance token. Not active.

**Committed order** - The large-trade venue. `commit_swap(side, direction, amount_in, min_out, nonce)` escrows the input, which fills at NAV ± `max(commit_spread_bps, 10)` bps at any keeper push after the commit. The keeper posts `clamp(half-spread, 10, 500)` bps. As of 10 Oct 2026 only QQQ (18), SPY (15) and XAU (18) accept committed orders. After the 180 s TTL, the owner can sign `cancel_swap` to refund an unfilled order. → [Trade flow](/flows/trade).

**Confidence interval** - The uncertainty band around a pushed price, stored as `Market.oracle_confidence`. On devnet it comes from a GJR-GARCH volatility model. Worst-case quoting widens mint and redeem quotes by it.

**Constant-product invariant** - `L_NAV × S_NAV = K × γ`, where `K = initial_l_price × initial_s_price` and γ is the short leg's volatility-decay index. As one leg rises the other falls.

**CPI** (Cross-Program Invocation) - Solana's mechanism for one program to call another. The CLP calls mint-redeem by CPI to mint and redeem pairs from its own vault (`vault_mint_pairs`, `vault_redeem_pairs`).

**cUSDC** - Continuum's devnet collateral mint (`B1c5xBYkp7AAemYhcu4VuH4CU4sPJDDuG2iuv6ts38uE`). On mainnet the collateral is real USDC. These docs say "cUSDC" to avoid confusion.

## D to L

**Drift gate (reposition gate)** - The move in a book's target centre that triggers a re-centre: 20 bps by default (`CLMM_REPOSITION_BPS`), cut further so the book moves before NAV reaches the floor of its first funded bin.

**Dynamic buffer** - The overcollateralization target the keeper posts per market (`set_buffer_target`): `B = δ²/2 + |q|·δ + margin`, with δ scaled to volatility and `q` fixed at 0.3. The keeper never posts below 102%; the program clamps any target to 101% to 150%.

**Fee waiver** - A grant created by `donate_to_vault` that lets the donor mint and redeem fee-free for a period, through `mint_paired_with_waiver` and `redeem_paired_with_waiver`. It applies only while the market's vault is above its target ratio. The app no longer offers it.

**Force price** - The oracle program's `force_price` instruction, compiled only into devnet builds. An operator emergency tool.

**Free float** - The book the keeper posts while mint and redeem are frozen (last push over 300 s old): anchored at the last price, inventory spread evenly, capped at $10,000 per side ($1,250 for single-name stocks). A guard can pull bins that other venues show have gone stale. → [Book management](/keeper/book-management).

**γ index** - `Market.s_gamma_index`, the short leg's volatility-decay index (1e9 = 1.0), which multiplies its NAV. The keeper lowers it as realized volatility accrues (`set_s_gamma_index`); the program rejects writes that raise it or fall out of range. `reanchor_gamma` (authority only, at least 14 days apart) folds γ into `initial_s_price` and resets it to 1. It is not a reset to the spot price. → [Volatility decay](/concepts/nav#volatility-decay).

**Guardian** - The freeze-only key on the CLP's `TreasuryPolicy`. It can freeze and unfreeze treasury withdrawals, cancel a pending loosening of the limits, and hand its role to a new key. It can't move funds. Only the guardian can unfreeze. → [Treasury](/programs/clp#treasury).

**`initial_l_price` / `initial_s_price`** - Anchors set when a market is initialized. Their product is K in the NAV formulas. NAV never falls back to them. `reanchor_gamma` folds γ into `initial_s_price`.

**Instant depth** - The book's standing bin liquidity: what a `pool_swap` can fill immediately. Deliberately lean.

**Inventory centre-shift** - The keeper's shift of each book's centre to work off the protocol's own inventory. When the public holds more L than S, the CLP holds the extra S, so the long book is quoted dearer and the short book cheaper. At most half the narrower leg's half-spread. On by default.

**`is_long`** - Boolean argument to the single-leg instructions (`keeper_mint_single`, `keeper_redeem_single`, `keeper_burn_single`, `permissionless_burn`). `true` means the long leg, `false` the short leg.

**Keeper** - The off-chain Rust service the protocol runs: price pushes, book management, committed-order settlement, inventory moves and parameter posting. Its source is not published. → [Keeper overview](/keeper/overview).

**`keeper_authority`** - The pubkey stored on `Market` that may push prices (`update_risk_state_v2`), post buffer targets and γ, rebalance collateral between markets, and call the keeper-only single-leg instructions.

**L** (Long) - One half of a Continuum paired position. Rises in value as the underlying rises.

**`L_NAV`** - Long-leg NAV: `Market.user_twap_price`, the spot price the keeper pushes, scaled to 1e6 and adjusted for corporate actions. If it is 0 or more than 300 s old, mint and redeem revert with `TwapNotAvailable`.

**LP** (Liquidity Provider) - The protocol is the only LP, through the CLP. There are no user LP shares.

## M to R

**`mint_fee_bps`** - Per-market mint fee, posted by the keeper as `clamp(fee_mult × half-spread, 10, 100)` bps. As of 10 Oct 2026: QQQ 18, SPY 16, NVDA 31, MSTR 52, EUR/GBP/JPY 10. The risk-state multiplier applies on top (1.5× in ProxyMode), and 10% of the fee is a dev tax.

**`mint_paired`** - User-facing instruction. Deposits at least 10 cUSDC and returns L and S at NAV, half the net value each. → [Mint flow](/flows/mint).

**Mint authority** - The signer that can mint new SPL tokens. For Continuum's L and S mints it is the Market PDA. There is no freeze authority.

**MVP** - The release deployed on devnet today. It has no governance and no user LP, and its collateral yield is emulated rather than earned.

**NAV** (Net Asset Value) - The on-chain protocol price for L and S, used by mint and redeem. → [NAV math](/concepts/nav).

**Native book / bin book** - Continuum's own per-leg quote book: one `Pool` account per market and side, up to 16 bins stored inline (no per-bin rent), filled by `pool_swap`. Each bin holds a fixed amount at a fixed price, and a swap walks outward from the centre. The `CLMM_*` keeper variables and `clmm.rs` keep an older label. → [What the book actually is](/concepts/clp#what-the-book-actually-is).

**OI** (Open Interest) - Outstanding token supply, tracked per leg on the `Market` account (`total_l_supply`, `total_s_supply`).

**`oi_cap`** - A cap stored on each market's `Clp` account (250,000 cUSDC on every live market as of 10 Oct 2026). It is not enforced: only the authority-only `update_oi` checks it, and nothing calls that.

**Oracle** - The oracle program, which holds a per-market `OracleConfig` with feeds, TWAPs and health state. On devnet the keeper does not update it; mint and redeem use the price the keeper writes to `Market`. → [Oracle concept](/concepts/oracle).

**Paired** - As in `mint_paired` and `redeem_paired`: the user-facing entry points, where L and S are minted or burned together at NAV-weighted values rather than in equal quantities.

**PDA** (Program Derived Address) - Solana's deterministic pubkey derived from seeds. Continuum has many: `Market`, `Clp`, `OracleConfig` and others. → [PDA derivations](/build/idls-and-pdas#pda-derivation-cheatsheet).

**Protective skew** - The spread engine's trend response: the side a continuing trend would hit widens with the drift z-score, and the other side never tightens below the symmetric shape. An antisymmetric variant, which also tightened the other side, failed in backtests.

**ProxyMode** - The risk state every devnet market runs in. The programs price from `Market.user_twap_price`, and fees and the confidence band are multiplied by 1.5. → [Risk states](/concepts/risk-states).

**Pyth** - The oracle network mainnet will use (Pyth Pro). On devnet the keeper prices markets from a free public quote source, because Pyth's public price endpoint now needs an API key.

**Realized ratchet** - The spread engine's fast term: a trailing empirical q90 of window moves, warm after about 8 windows. One of four terms in the half-spread.

**Recovery** - The risk state after Stress. The fee and confidence multiplier falls from 3× to 1× over the hour after the market enters Recovery.

**`redeem_fee_bps`** - Per-market redeem fee. The keeper posts the same value as `mint_fee_bps`.

**`redeem_paired`** - User-facing instruction. Burns L and S, both amounts above zero and worth at least 10 cUSDC together, and returns cUSDC at NAV less the redeem fee. → [Redeem flow](/flows/redeem).

**Registry** - The on-chain symbol-to-market index (`Hss7QZ46RcrV4ta63q2EM2Wf1Pzbjnd5FNjaTTDZHnau`). It is deployed and initialized, but it holds no markets and nothing reads it yet. Its only venue type is `MeteoraDlmm`. → [Registry program](/programs/registry).

**`risk_state`** - `Normal`, `ProxyMode`, `Stress` or `Recovery`, stored on `Market` and written by the keeper with `update_risk_state_v2`. Devnet markets are always in `ProxyMode`. Fee and confidence multipliers: 1×, 1.5×, 3×, and 3× falling to 1× over 1 hour. → [Risk states](/concepts/risk-states).

## S to Z

**S** (Short) - One half of a Continuum paired position. Rises in value as the underlying falls.

**`S_NAV`** - Short-leg NAV: `K / L_NAV × s_gamma_index / 1e9`, with `K = initial_l_price × initial_s_price`.

**Slippage** - The difference between the expected and the actual swap output. On the bin books it is the real cost of walking the bin ladder. Committed orders have none: they fill at NAV ± `max(commit_spread_bps, 10)` bps whatever their size.

**Slow auto-floor** - The spread engine's slow term: `floor_mult × σ_60d`, never below 5 bps. It needs about 10 days of history in memory to engage, and the static floor applies before then.

**Solvency invariant** - A property the protocol enforces at all times. → [Solvency invariants](/concepts/solvency).

**Stress** - The risk state for extreme confidence or staleness. Mint and redeem are both blocked by default: `allow_mint_in_stress` and `allow_redeem_in_stress` are false on every live market.

**Treasury** - The CLP's global vault, the cUSDC ATA of the `GlobalClp` PDA (`5GSTKN4u5LfropcnQQoqFht48JMaF1SzZzBcgDNL5VKy`). It held 18,524,820.82 cUSDC on 11 Oct 2026, after the keeper's liquidity reallocator returned idle CLP cash to it. `backstop_market` moves it into a market's collateral with no limit. `admin_withdraw` takes it out of the protocol at a capped rate, set in `TreasuryPolicy`, to one named account. The keeper's yield allocator counts it as first-loss capital. → [Treasury](/programs/clp#treasury).

**TWAP** (Time-Weighted Average Price) - A price averaged over a window. The oracle program keeps TWAPs in `OracleConfig`, but nothing updates them on devnet. `Market.user_twap_price` is a spot price despite its name.

**Two-zone geometry** - The book's bin layout: a fine core step near the centre for tight instant pricing, and wide wing steps beyond `core_bins`, so a free-float book covers large off-hours moves with few bins.

**`user_twap_price`** - The `Market` field that drives `L_NAV`. The keeper writes it with mint-redeem `update_risk_state_v2`, along with `twap_updated_at`.

**Vault** (collateral vault) - The token account holding a market's backing cUSDC (`market.collateral_vault`). Every market has its own: the cUSDC ATA of its Market PDA. QQQ, SPY and XAU shared the unified vault (PDA `[b"unified_vault"]`, `5a51yurKcqxy6rVAV1ykif5fDzhHdjgZs3EL7yncLrp8`) until 11 Oct 2026. It holds 0 cUSDC, and that day's upgrade removed it from the program.

**Venue split** - Routing by size: bin books for instant trades on all 14 markets, and committed orders plus the instant oracle swap (`clp_swap`) on QQQ, SPY and XAU. All price off the same keeper-pushed NAV.

**Worst-case quoting** - Mint and redeem price each leg against the user by `c = oracle_confidence × state multiplier`: mint values L at P + c and S at `K / (P − c) × γ`, redeem L at P − c and S at `K / (P + c) × γ`. A per-market switch (`worst_case_config.enabled`), on everywhere except QQQ as of 10 Oct 2026.

**Yield allocator** - The keeper task that decides how much of each market's collateral to lend, and where, from live mainnet venue state. Each venue's total position across all markets is capped at first-loss capital divided by the venue failures to cover (1 by default). First-loss capital is the treasury plus half of all markets' collateral above 100%. It lends only on mainnet with `COLLATERAL_YIELD_ENABLED=true`. On devnet an emulator credits the interest its plan would earn. Every live market has `yield_enabled = true` as of 11 Oct 2026, and new markets start with it true. Harvested yield is added to the market's own collateral.
