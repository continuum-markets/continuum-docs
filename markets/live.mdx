Thirteen markets are live on Solana devnet, with 26 bin books between them. Together they hold about \$1.37M of collateral, the sum of `Market.total_collateral` on 12 Oct 2026 after the protocol's own holdings were cut to working size (see below). Mainnet markets will be added when mainnet launches. MSTR was wound down and paused on 12 Oct 2026 (see [MSTR](#mstr-microstrategy)). OIL was withdrawn on 30 Aug 2026 and VXX was delisted in June 2026, so neither appears here.

<Note>
  The cUSDC mint for all devnet markets is `B1c5xBYkp7AAemYhcu4VuH4CU4sPJDDuG2iuv6ts38uE`. On mainnet the collateral will be USDC.
</Note>

On 12 Oct 2026 the CLP's own holdings, which had been about \$16.6M across these markets, were cut first to about \$50,000 a leg per market and then, the same day, to 0.5% of each market's claims per leg, the share the keeper's nightly job now re-derives. The books use \$5,000 to \$10,000 a side, and committed orders mint what they need when they settle. The CLP redeemed the rest at NAV with `vault_redeem_single`, so no holder's tokens changed value, and \$16.9M of collateral went back to the treasury. Most of what remains sits in the bin books. Each book was left balanced between its long and short values. QQQ, SPY and XAU hold more because some of their long tokens sit outside the CLP, and the CLP keeps short tokens to offset them.

Every market set `initial_l_price` and `initial_s_price` to the same value, so its constant K is that price squared. No market has been re-anchored yet.

The keeper posts one fee as both the mint fee and the redeem fee. All devnet markets run in ProxyMode, which charges 1.5× the posted fee, so QQQ's 18 bps costs 27 bps.

Committed orders fill at NAV plus or minus the market's committed spread, shown for QQQ, SPY and XAU. The keeper re-posts each spread as volatility moves, so read `Clp.commit_spread_bps` for the current value. Since 11 Oct 2026 committed orders are on for every market except MSTR, and they are the only way to trade while a market's price is live. MSTR was wound down and paused on 12 Oct 2026 (see [MSTR](#mstr-microstrategy)). The bin books trade only while a market's price is stale, and the CLP's instant `clp_swap` was retired on 11 Oct 2026. The JPY market tracks USD/JPY, so `JPYL` gains when the yen weakens against the dollar.

Each market's `Clp` stores an OI cap of 250,000 cUSDC, which nothing enforces yet. The bin books charge no swap fee.

On devnet the keeper prices every market from a public quote source. Before each push it checks the price against the Hyperliquid market named in that market's table below, and a disagreement blocks the push. Hyperliquid can veto a price but never sets one. Mainnet will use Pyth. See [Oracle](/concepts/oracle).

## Programmatic access

Derive the PDAs from the market symbol rather than copying them by hand. [Address registry](/reference/addresses#derive-addresses-from-a-symbol) has a sample that returns every PDA and pool vault for a symbol. The mints, the collateral vault and the fee recipient aren't PDAs; read them from the Market account (`longMint`, `shortMint`, `collateralVault`, `feeRecipient`).

## Trading hours

Mint and redeem aren't 24/7. They revert with `TwapNotAvailable` once the keeper's last price is more than 300 s old, and the keeper pushes no prices while the underlying is closed. The bin books are the reverse: they fill only while mint and redeem are frozen, and the keeper then caps each of the seven single-stock books at \$1,250 a side. While prices are pushing, trades go through committed orders.
